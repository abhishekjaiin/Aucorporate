## Recurring Entities & Regulations
Named/cited consistently across ranking pages:
- **FEMA 22(R)/2016-RB** — Foreign Exchange Management (Establishment in India of a Branch Office or a Liaison Office or a Project Office or Any Other Place of Business) Regulations, 2016 (confirmed as the operative regulation per prior pipeline research; also surfaced independently here via incometaxindia.gov.in and rbidocs.rbi.org.in results).
- **Regulation 5** (cited by some sources as "Regulation 5(ii)," referencing older Notification No. 22/2000-RB and FEMA 95/2003 language) — general permission conditions for a Project Office: (a) project funded by inward remittance from abroad, or (b) funded by a bilateral/multilateral international financing agency, or (c) the project cleared by an appropriate authority, or (d) the Indian company/entity awarding the contract has been granted a term loan by a public financial institution or bank in India for the project. **Note:** sources are inconsistent about whether the current FEMA 22(R)/2016 renumbers this as "Regulation 5" verbatim or carries it forward from the 2000/2003 regulations under a different clause number — needs primary-source confirmation (RBI/MCA domains were unreachable this run).
- **Form FNC / Form FNC-1** — cited as the application/reporting form via the AD Category-I bank. Sources conflict on exact usage (see Open Questions).
- **AD Category-I Bank** — the routing authority for both the general-permission (deemed/reporting) route and the specific-approval route to RBI Central Office.
- **RBI Regional Office** — recipient (via AD bank) of the PO eligibility undertaking/report under general permission, per one source, within 2 months of establishment.
- **Companies Act, 2013, Section 380** and **Rule 3(3)/3(4) of the Companies (Registration of Foreign Companies) Rules, 2014** — MCA/ROC registration obligation for any foreign company with a "place of business" in India, via **Form FC-1** (initial registration, within 30 days of establishment) and **Form FC-2** (alterations/closure notification).
- **Form FC-1** — ROC registration form for PO (and BO/LO) establishment.
- **Form FC-2** — used for reporting changes and, per some sources, for closure/cessation notification to ROC.
- **PAN, TAN** — obtained post-registration.
- **Annual Activity Certificate (AAC)** — filed annually (as at 31 March) with the AD Category-I bank and Director-General of Income Tax (International Taxation), New Delhi, per Form FNC Annex D per one source.
- **FLA Return, ITR-6** — recurring annual compliance items mentioned alongside AAC.
- **Permanent Establishment (PE)** — Income Tax Act, 1961 concept and DTAA "Construction PE"/"Installation PE" (typically 6- or 12-month threshold per specific treaty) and general "fixed place of business" / service-PE (90-day) tests — recur specifically in the tax-focused sources (bcajonline.org, taxsutra, ahlawatassociates.com, akmglobal.com) as the dominant tax-risk framing for project offices specifically (distinct from LO, which carries much lower PE risk because it cannot conduct commercial activity).
- **Sectoral clearances / prior-approval triggers** — applicants from Pakistan; applicants from Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Afghanistan, Iran, China, Hong Kong, Macau seeking to set up in North-East India, J&K, or Andaman & Nicobar; or entities in Defence, Private Security, Telecom, or Information & Broadcasting sectors — these trigger RBI Central Office prior approval rather than the AD-bank/general-permission route (per vjmglobal.com synthesis; not independently verified against primary text this run).
- **No UIN for PO** — asserted by at least one source (Reema Jain & Associates comparison synthesis) as a distinguishing feature vs BO/LO. **Directly contradicted** by another source (a different WebSearch synthesis citing "RBI will... issue a Unique Identification Number (UIN) for the Project Office"). This is the single most important factual conflict for the next agent to resolve — see Open Questions.

## Orchestrator follow-up (2026-09-22) — resolving the UIN conflict flagged in Open Question #1
Ran 2 additional targeted WebSearch queries to try to resolve the UIN contradiction. Findings:
- RBI hosts a form literally named `form-fnc1.pdf` at rbi.org.in/Upload/Forms/Pdfs/form-fnc1.pdf, titled "FNC 1 (See Regulation 5)" — this is a real signal (RBI's own domain/filename) that Project Offices use a **distinct form, "Form FNC-1"**, specifically tied to Regulation 5 (the general-permission route), separate from the "Form FNC" used for Branch/Liaison Office applications.
- Multiple independent sources (taxguru.in process-establishment-project-offices-india-foreign-entities.html, masllp.com, gkkediaandco.com, carajput.com) now converge on: a copy of the Form FNC(-1) is sent to RBI's General Manager for **UIN allotment**, same as BO/LO — directly contradicting the "no UIN for PO" claim Stage 1 also found.

**Correction to this pipeline's carried-forward premise:** STATUS.md's "known facts" section stated as settled that "a Project Office does NOT require RBI to issue a UIN the same way a Branch or Liaison Office does" — carried forward loosely from the Branch/Liaison Office pipelines' own pages, which say POs don't get a UIN "in the same way." Fresh, more targeted research this session suggests this may be **wrong or at least incomplete** — the weight of evidence now leans toward POs also receiving a UIN via Form FNC-1/Regulation 5, not being UIN-exempt. **This is still not primary-source confirmed** (WebFetch to rbi.org.in remains blocked; all of this is WebSearch synthesis, including of RBI's own PDF filename/title, not a direct read of its content).
