## Recurring Entities & Regulations
Named consistently across ranking pages/snippets:
- **MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs)** — the incorporating authority; SPICe+ portal named explicitly and repeatedly.
- **SPICe+ (Form INC-32)** — named on nearly every process-focused page as the single incorporation form (Part A name reservation, Part B incorporation); linked forms AGILE-PRO-S and INC-9 also named.
- **RUN (Reserve Unique Name)** — named specifically for the name-reservation step (superseded/paired with SPICe+ Part A in current process — some sources may be describing an older workflow; worth Agent 3 checking currency).
- **DIN (Director Identification Number)** and **DSC (Digital Signature Certificate — Class 3)** — named on every process page.
- **PAN, TAN, GSTIN** — named as auto-issued/linked via SPICe+/AGILE-PRO-S.
- **FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act)** — named as the governing framework for foreign investment compliance.
- **RBI (Reserve Bank of India)** — named for FDI reporting post-incorporation (Form FC-GPR on the FIRMS portal, within 30 days of share allotment) and for approval-route sectors.
- **FDI Automatic Route vs Government/Approval Route** — a recurring conceptual split across nearly every page; sector-dependent.
- **Companies Act, 2013** — named specifically, including **Section 149(3)** (resident director requirement — 182 days) and **Section 164** (director disqualification).
- **DTAA (Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement), India-US** — named on multiple pages with specific detail: signed 1989, effective 1991; withholding tax on dividends reduced to 15% (≥10% holding) or 25% below that threshold; royalties 10–15%; Foreign Tax Credit (US Form 1118) as the US-side relief mechanism; Permanent Establishment (PE) concept.
- **FC-1** — named for foreign company (branch/liaison/project office) registration specifically, distinct from SPICe+ subsidiary incorporation.
- **FBAR / FinCEN Form 114** — named as a US-side compliance trigger once a US person has signature authority over a foreign (Indian) bank account exceeding $10,000.
- **EPFO, ESIC, Professional Tax** — named as post-incorporation/employment-related registrations bundled into SPICe+.
- Entity types named repeatedly: **Private Limited Company (most recommended), LLP, Wholly Owned Subsidiary, Branch Office, Liaison Office, Project Office, One Person Company (OPC — India-residents only, not directly relevant to US founders), sole proprietorship/partnership (flagged as requiring RBI approval, generally discouraged for foreign nationals).**

## Search Intent (page-level read)
- **Predominantly informational/how-to**, even on pages published by firms that sell the service. Nearly every ranking page observed is structured as an educational guide (requirements → entity choice → process steps → documents → timeline → cost → FAQ) rather than a hard-sell service page. This matches classic "top of funnel, high-consideration B2B service" behavior — evidence: article-style titles and long-form "Complete Guide" framing dominate (masllp.com explicitly titles its page "Complete 2026 Guide"; companyformationindia.com and indiafilings.com use "How to..." titles).
- **Commercial intent is present but secondary** — most of these guide pages sit on service-selling domains (CA firms, registration platforms) and almost certainly end in a CTA/consultation pitch, but the ranking content itself leads with education, not offer. This is evidence for **mixed informational-commercial intent**: Google is rewarding pages that answer the process/requirements question thoroughly, not pages that lead with a sales pitch.
- A smaller number of pages read as more purely commercial/service-page in nature (companiesnext.com's non-blog `/company-registration-in-india-from-usa` URL, beaconfiling.com's `/country-pages/` template, indiafilings.com's core service offering) — these look built to convert rather than to rank on comprehensiveness, though I could not verify their actual on-page structure (fetch blocked).
- **No purely transactional intent** observed (e.g., a "buy now / instant checkout" flow) — this is a considered-purchase, consultation-led category, consistent with AU Corporate's own model.
- **No navigational intent** signals relevant here (nobody appears to be searching for a specific brand name in these queries).
