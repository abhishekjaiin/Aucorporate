## Search Intent Assessment
**Shifted from what the page assumed, though not newly shifted — the page was simply built for a narrower intent than what actually exists.** The live page treated "HR Services" as a pure service-menu/lead-gen page (three tiles, a CTA). The real query space (payroll outsourcing India, HR outsourcing India, EOR India, PF/ESI compliance for a foreign subsidiary) is a mix of purely commercial intent (foreign companies ready to buy) and upper-funnel informational/decision intent (foreign companies still working out whether they need an EOR, when to incorporate, what payroll compliance actually costs them in obligations). The original page only served the first group and only shallowly. The expanded page now serves both without diluting the commercial framing — the EOR-vs-own-entity section and FAQ are decision-support for the informational-intent segment, but every section still funnels back to a "talk to an expert" / lead-form CTA.

No pricing figures were invented for the "how much does payroll outsourcing cost" FAQ; it's answered qualitatively with a CTA to get a sized quote instead.
