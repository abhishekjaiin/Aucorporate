## Edits Made

1. **Quick Answer opening sentence** (Section 1): Changed "Registering a company in India for a foreign parent generally means..." → "Company registration in India for a foreign parent generally means..." — puts the exact primary keyword phrase at the very start of body copy instead of a close variant, satisfying the "primary keyword in first ~100 words" checklist item without altering meaning or reading awkwardly.

2. **Section 2 disambiguation paragraph** ("Company Registration vs. Company Incorporation"): Added "company formation" as a third synonym alongside "company registration" and "company incorporation," since this term is an explicitly designated secondary keyword ("keep as an on-page synonym, not abandoned") that was otherwise absent from the body text entirely. This section is literally about interchangeable terminology, so the addition is on-topic and reads naturally rather than as an inserted keyword.

3. **Internal link anchor + surrounding phrasing** (end of "What Happens After Incorporation" section): Changed the `/india-business-setup/regulatory-compliance` anchor from generic "regulatory compliance" to "full regulatory compliance framework," matching Stage 6's suggested anchor text more closely. Also changed "pages document in full" → "pages cover in detail" in the same sentence to avoid stacking three uses of "full" in three consecutive clauses (anchor + "document in full" + "reading in full"), which the anchor change would otherwise have introduced.

No other changes made. Metadata (title, meta description, URL) left untouched — already locked in from Stage 6 and already compliant.
