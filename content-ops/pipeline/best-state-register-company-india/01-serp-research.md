## Recurring Entities & Regulations
- **MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs)** / **RoC (Registrar of Companies)** — India has 25 RoC offices; registration is filed with the RoC of the state where the registered office sits (Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu each have two RoCs). This is the one place the topic is genuinely *not* optional/flexible: you don't "choose" an RoC independent of where your registered office is.
- **Stamp duty** — repeatedly named as the one cost figure that genuinely varies by state (it's a State List subject under the Indian Constitution), applied to the MOA/AOA at incorporation. Cited range: ~₹123 (Himachal Pradesh) to ~₹10,025 (Punjab) at ₹1 lakh authorised capital in one source; a second source gave a different worked example at ₹25 lakh authorised capital (Maharashtra ₹5,200 vs. Karnataka ₹2,250 vs. Gujarat ₹12,600) — **these two sources partially disagree on which states are "cheap" at different capital levels**, which itself is a useful finding: state stamp-duty ranking is not stable across capital bands, so any comparison content needs to show the math, not just a static ranking.
- **Professional Tax (PT)** — a separate State List tax under Article 276 of the Constitution (capped at ₹2,500/person/year), governed by separate state legislation (e.g., Maharashtra State Tax on Professions Act 1975, Karnataka's 1976 Act, West Bengal's 1979 Act). Several states don't levy it at all. PT liability follows the *employee's work location*, not the state of incorporation — a materially different rule than stamp duty, and a common source of confusion worth calling out explicitly if this topic is built.
- **NITI Aayog Investment Friendliness Index 2026** — cited as ranking Gujarat #1, Maharashtra #2, Tamil Nadu #3.
- **DPIIT BRAP (Business Reforms Action Plan)** — seven editions cited (2015, 2016, 2017-18, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2024-in-progress), the standard government ease-of-doing-business-by-state benchmark.
- **CareEdge State Ranking Report 2025** — cited naming Maharashtra top for financial services/capital markets.
- **MIDC (Maharashtra Industrial Development Corporation)** — named as the state industrial-development-corporation model for land/infrastructure facilitation.
- **GIFT City / IFSC (International Financial Services Centres Authority)** — recurs heavily in adjacent searches: single regulator (combining RBI/SEBI/IRDAI/PFRDA powers for IFSC), stamp duty exemption, 10-year tax holiday (any 10 of 15 years), lower 9% MAT for banks. This is a distinct, higher-intensity sub-topic (a specific zone, not a general state comparison) that came up independently enough to flag as a possible separate cluster/page rather than a section here.
- **SEZ (Special Economic Zones)** — duty-free imports, single-window clearance, recurs as a general state-incentive lever (not state-specific itself).
- **PLI (Production-Linked Incentive) Scheme** — federal, not state, but recurs alongside state manufacturing-incentive content (Gujarat/Tamil Nadu manufacturing incentives specifically named: Gujarat's 12-25% capital subsidies, 100% SGST reimbursement for priority sectors, 207 industrial estates).
- **FEMA / RBI** — appears mainly as background (post-incorporation compliance is federal, not state-driven) rather than as part of the state-choice decision itself — worth confirming this distinction explicitly in any content built, since AU's existing india-business-setup pages already own the FEMA/RBI/DPIIT-route territory and this topic should not re-litigate it.
