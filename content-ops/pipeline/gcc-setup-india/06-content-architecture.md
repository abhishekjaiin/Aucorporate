Flag this tradeoff explicitly for the human: the page will still be more specific/credible than competitors on compliance mechanics, but will not out-credential KNM India's public team-page signal on authorship alone.

## Recommended Content Type
**Durable pillar/guide page** (not a blog post, not a Big-4-style service landing page). Anchored at its own top-level URL (matching the site's existing `/india-business-setup`, `/doing-business-in-india`, and `/india-entry-for-us-companies` pattern — a stable hub URL, not a dated `/blog/` post), with structure that anticipates linking out to future cluster posts.

**Why:** Stage 1's SERP intent read is "informational-led with commercial undertone" — the dominant ranking format across 15 competitors is a long-form "how to / what is / cost of" guide, not a dated blog article or a bare "talk to us" service page (that's the Big 4 pattern, and Stage 5 explicitly positions AU Corp against the Big 4's higher price point, not alongside their landing-page format). Stage 3's cross-page pattern and Stage 4's "Minimum Coverage List" both independently confirm that every strong-performing competitor (KNM India, TheIntechGroup, Wisemonk, VJM Global) runs a **pillar-plus-cluster architecture**: one comprehensive guide plus tightly-scoped companion pages, not one page trying to cover everything and not a single blog post. The human's scope decision for this run (pillar only, cluster deferred) fits this format exactly — build the pillar to be genuinely load-bearing on its own per Stage 1's finding that competitors' single strongest pages are still comprehensive, while structuring headings so cost/cities/entity-comparison/timeline sub-topics can be "promoted" into their own cluster pages later without restructuring the pillar.
