## New Authoritative Information Found
Re-verified in a follow-up pass (same session, after the initial edit was interrupted by a transient rate-limit error and later resumed): pulled current GSC data for this exact URL (9 impressions, 0 clicks, avg. position 68.7, trailing 90 days — unchanged from the figures below, confirming the earlier pull was accurate) and re-checked Form INC-20A and SPICe+/MCA V3 mechanics against current sources. No rate/deadline/form change since this page was last written — the underlying law is stable, but the page's *coverage* of that law was incomplete:
- **Companies Act, 2013, Section 149(3)** — resident director requirement (182+ days in the relevant period, non-continuous days permitted) — confirmed via multiple independent secondary sources describing the same statutory text; page previously didn't explain the mechanism at all, only referenced it in passing.
- **Companies Act, 2013, Section 149(1)** — minimum two directors, minimum two shareholders for a private limited company — not previously stated anywhere on the page.
- **Companies (Amendment) Act, 2015** — removed the earlier ₹1 lakh minimum paid-up capital requirement — not previously stated; a common point of confusion for foreign founders comparing India to jurisdictions with capital minimums.
- **MCA V3 portal / SPICe+ / AGILE-PRO-S** — SPICe+ is the single integrated incorporation filing on the MCA's V3 portal, with the linked AGILE-PRO-S form covering EPFO, ESIC, GST, and bank-account details — page previously said "SPICe+ Part B" without naming the linked form or portal.
- **Form INC-22 / registered office timing** — SPICe+ permits filing against a correspondence address with the registered office confirmed to the ROC within 30 days of incorporation — not previously mentioned; relevant because many US founders don't have an Indian address finalized before they want to start filing.
- Confirmed a US LLC (not only a US C-corp) can directly hold shares in an Indian private limited company under the Automatic Route — not previously addressed, and a real question US founders using an LLC parent structure have.
- **Form INC-20A (Section 10A, Companies Act, 2013)** — declaration of commencement of business, due within 180 days of incorporation; applies to companies incorporated on or after 2 November 2018 (all current incorporations); non-filing risks the ROC striking the company off, and the late fee escalates up to 12x the normal fee. Confirmed via TaxGuru and current filing-guide sources; consistent with the identical fact already stated on the UK sibling page. Not previously mentioned anywhere on this page — added in the follow-up pass.
- Checked for any 2026-specific procedural changes to SPICe+/MCA V3: confirmed SPICe+ (Form INC-32) remains the sole incorporation route on MCA V3 for FY 2026-27, with typical Certificate of Incorporation turnaround of 7-10 working days when documents are in order. Noted but did **not** add to the page: a nationwide ROC-jurisdiction reorganisation effective 16 February 2026 and a new geo-tagged-photo requirement for *annual filings* — both are real 2026 MCA developments but affect post-incorporation/ongoing compliance, not the incorporation steps this page covers, so adding them here would be scope creep against a targeted refresh.

None of this changes anything already stated elsewhere on the site (the FEMA/FC-GPR 30-day deadline and apostille framing already on this page and on the cost-timeline page remain accurate and were left untouched).
