## New Authoritative Information Found
1. **Singapore's FDI-leadership streak is now eight consecutive years, not seven.** DPIIT data released June 2026 confirms Singapore remained India's largest FDI-equity source in FY2025-26 ($19.8B of $58.85B total, 33.6%), extending a run that has held since FY2018-19. The page's "seven consecutive years" language was accurate as of the June 2025 data release (FY2024-25) but is now one year stale. **Updated** to "eight consecutive years," with the current cumulative figure (~$194.7B since April 2000, per DPIIT) replacing the rounder "approaching $195 billion."
2. **The page's Limitation of Benefits (LOB) card contained an inaccurate description of Article 24A.** The live page described a third LOB test — "under 50% of gross income paid to non-residents with at least 50% beneficial ownership held in India or Singapore" — that does not appear in the actual India-Singapore DTAA Article 24A (2005 Protocol, amended 2016). Cross-checked against multiple independent tax-advisory sources (search-verified; primary-source incometaxindia.gov.in and several secondary domains were blocked by the network egress proxy and could not be fetched directly) which consistently describe only two tests: a **stock exchange listing test** and a **bona fide business/expenditure test** (SGD 200,000, or the INR 50 lakh equivalent, of operating expenditure in Singapore in *each* of the two 12-month periods within the 24 months preceding the date the gain arises). Also confirmed Article 24A applies specifically to Article 13 capital-gains benefits, not the treaty as a whole — a scope distinction the original card didn't make. **Corrected** the card and added a dedicated section with a comparison table and this scope clarification.
3. **The Supreme Court's Tiger Global ruling (Civil Appeal No. 262 of 2026, decided 15 January 2026)** is now a live, citable precedent — already referenced by the site's own blog post but not connected from this page in a substantively accurate way. Confirmed via multiple sources (india-briefing, DLA Piper, Supreme Court Observer, Legal500) that the ruling: was decided under the India-Mauritius treaty (not India-Singapore) but on "substance over form" / GAAR-supremacy reasoning that Indian authorities are now applying across treaty structures generally; held that a valid TRC is not conclusive; and confirmed GAAR can override treaty provisions including grandfathering clauses. **Added** this with the correct scope caveat (Mauritius treaty, not Singapore) rather than implying it was decided under the Singapore DTAA.
4. **Singapore's fund/asset-management scale is citable and current**: MAS's 2025 Asset Management Survey (reported August 2026) — S$6.7 trillion (~US$5.2 trillion) total AUM, ~US$622 billion in PE/VC strategies, 1,406 VCCs and 3,443 sub-funds registered. Used as sourced, specific statistics rather than invented figures for the new PE/VC gateway section.
5. Reverified DTAA withholding rates (10%/15% dividends by 25% shareholding threshold, 10%/15% interest by lender type, flat 10% royalties/FTS) — **unchanged**, confirmed still current, no edit needed.
