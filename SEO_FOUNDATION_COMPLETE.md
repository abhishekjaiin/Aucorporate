## Content Specifications

### Per-Page Requirements
- **Word Count:** 2,500-3,500 words minimum
- **Heading Structure:** H1 + minimum 5 H2 sections
- **Internal Links:** 5-8 related service/guide links
- **Schema Markup:** Breadcrumb + Service + FAQ + Organization
- **SEO Elements:** Title (50-60 chars), Meta (150-160 chars), FAQs
- **User Features:** Table of Contents, Accordion FAQs, Related Services
