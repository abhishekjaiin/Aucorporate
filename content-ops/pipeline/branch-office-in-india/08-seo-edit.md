## Edits Made
1. **Intro sentence — primary keyword placement.** Changed "A Branch Office is one of the narrower, more specific ways a foreign company can establish a presence in India" to "A Branch Office in India is one of the narrower, more specific ways a foreign company can establish a presence here" (and removed the now-redundant "in India" later in the same sentence, replacing "represent the parent's interests here" phrasing accordingly). Reason: gets the exact-match primary keyword into the first sentence naturally, satisfying the "first ~100 words" checklist item without adding a new clause or changing meaning.
2. **Meta description shortened.** Original ran ~206 characters (Stage 6's doc claimed 153 characters, which doesn't hold up on an actual character count) — well past the point Google typically truncates SERP snippets (~155–160 characters). Trimmed to 155 characters by removing "the" before "Automatic vs Approval route" and cutting the closing clause "— explained with current regulatory citations." Every named hook (RBI/FEMA 22(R), eligibility, Automatic vs Approval route, Form FNC, permitted activities, tax treatment, closure) and the primary keyword are retained. This is a length/technical fix, not a factual or content change.
3. **Updated the Metadata section's own annotation** to reflect the meta description edit and its reasoning (previously stated "no tweaks required" for all three fields; now accurately scoped to title and URL only).
4. **FAQ headings converted from bold pseudo-headings to true H3s.** All 12 FAQ items previously used `**1. Question?**` (bold text, not a heading tag) with the answer directly below. Converted each to `### 1. Question?` (kept the numbering for scan continuity). No answer text changed. Reason: real heading markup is the correct, more reliable signal for FAQ-rich-result eligibility and for correct document heading hierarchy — this was a genuine, low-risk structural gap against the checklist, not a style preference.

No other changes were made. The draft was already close to publish-ready on SEO fundamentals — keyword coverage, internal linking, intent-ordering, and FAQ answer quality all matched the Stage 6 blueprint closely enough that further edits would have been over-optimization for its own sake.

- **FAQ 1 ("Who approves a branch office in India — RBI or MCA?")** — per Stage 4/6, this is flagged as the single most differentiated FAQ answer on the page (few competitors disambiguate RBI vs MCA cleanly in one place); now correctly headed as an H3 with the direct answer immediately following, which is the right shape for a PAA/FAQ-rich-result pickup.

Not changed here since the year suffix is a deliberate Stage 6 differentiation decision; flagging for awareness only — writer/strategist call on whether the freshness signal is worth the truncation risk.
2. **CFC/DTAA jargon not expanded on this page.** "CFC rules, DTAA treatment" (Section 2 body) and "CFC/DTAA purposes" (FAQ 3) are used without expansion. This is deliberate — the page explicitly states it does not cover home-country tax consequences and routes readers to the country-specific comparison pages — so I did not add an explanation that would duplicate content those pages already own. Flagging only in case the writer wants a one-clause gloss (e.g., "Controlled Foreign Company rules") for readers who don't click through.
3. **Duplicate CTA text.** The inline CTA after "How AU Corporate Supports..." and the standalone "## CTA" block near the end of the file use near-identical copy. This reads as intentional content-ops structure (the "## CTA" block likely maps to a distinct LeadForm/component slot, similar to how "## Metadata" isn't page body copy), not a genuine on-page duplication — but worth a quick confirmation from whoever builds the page that only one of these renders in the actual reader-facing flow.
