## Recurring Entities & Regulations
Named, specifically, across multiple independently-ranking pages:
- **Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008** — governing statute (recurs everywhere; in force from 31-03-2009 per one source)
- **LLP (Amendment) Act, 2021** — amended the resident-partner test from 182 days to 120 days in the preceding financial year; this is the **current** rule (see Freshness Signals — many pages still show the outdated 182-day figure)
- **Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA)** / **MCA21 / MCA V3 portal** — registering authority
- **Form FiLLiP** (Form for Incorporation of LLP) — the incorporation form; MCA also references "Form 17" language in one official instruction-kit document, and RUN-LLP for name reservation
- **Form 3** — for filing the LLP Agreement (within 30 days of incorporation)
- **Form 8** (Statement of Account & Solvency, due 30 October) and **Form 11** (Annual Return, due 30 May)
- **Designated Partner Identification Number (DPIN)** — now merged/interchangeable with **DIN** (Director Identification Number)
- **Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)** — required per designated partner
- **LLP Agreement** — the foundational governing document, filed via Form 3
- **FEMA** — foreign nationals/NRI partners must comply with FEMA rules for their contribution
- **FDI automatic route**, specifically: **100% FDI in LLPs permitted under automatic route only in sectors/activities where 100% FDI is allowed under automatic route AND there are no FDI-linked performance conditions** — this exact two-part gate is stated consistently across multiple sources (IndiaFilings, VJM Global, Academy Tax4Wealth, QuickCompany) and traces to the **10 November 2015** FDI policy liberalization (before which LLP investment needed government approval). This is a narrower gate than standard company FDI and a recurring point of explanation across ranking pages — confirms it is a genuine differentiator worth covering precisely.
- **Section 10(2A), Income-tax Act** — exempts a partner's share of LLP profit from further tax in their hands (single-level taxation)
- **Section 366, Companies Act, 2013** + **Company (Authorised to Register) Rules, 2014** — governs LLP-to-company conversion; filings cited: **Form URC-1**, **SPICe+**, **INC-23**, **INC-24**
- **Alternate Minimum Tax (AMT)** — 18.5% referenced for LLPs claiming certain deductions
- Turnover/contribution audit threshold: **₹40 lakh turnover or ₹25 lakh partner contribution** triggers mandatory audit (recurs consistently across compliance-focused pages — treat as market consensus figure; not independently verified against the bare statute text, tool access to a primary source was blocked)

## Freshness Signals
- Several currently-ranking pages state the **pre-2021 residency test (182 days)** as if still current (e.g., IndiaFilings eligibility page per synthesized summary, and general "182 days" framing that shows up in multiple generic explainer pages) — the **correct current rule is 120 days in the preceding financial year**, per the LLP (Amendment) Act, 2021, confirmed by a dedicated SetIndiaBiz comparison piece ("Resident Director vs Designated Partner: 182 or 120 Days?") that exists specifically because of this confusion in the market. **This is a concrete, verifiable accuracy gap AU Corporate can win on.**
- The Clifford Chance briefing on FDI in LLPs is dated **2011** — pre-dates the 10 November 2015 liberalization to 100% automatic-route FDI; badly stale if anyone cites it as current guidance, though it did not rank in the primary "LLP registration" query set (surfaced only under the FDI-specific query).
- Several results explicitly reference **"2026"** or **"FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27"** in titles (LegalSuvidha "LLP vs Pvt Ltd in India 2026," Vakilsearch "Advantages and Disadvantages of LLP (2026 Guide)," SetIndiaBiz "LLP Annual Compliance FY 2025-26," Income Tax Dept "Partnership Firm/LLP for AY 2026-27") — the market is actively refreshing dated year-stamped content annually; a page without a current-year stamp will read as stale by comparison.
- Compliance deadlines cited are FY-specific and recur consistently (Form 11 by 30 May, Form 8 by 30 October) — these are evergreen annual dates, not tied to a single year, but pages should still carry a visible "last updated" or FY reference to compete with the 2026-dated set above.
- Could not independently confirm literal "last updated" timestamps on any individual competitor page — WebFetch was blocked for every domain tested; this is a genuine data gap, flagged rather than assumed.
