## Primary Keyword
**LLP registration in India** — intent: commercial/transactional (with an informational on-ramp), volume: Search volume not verified, target: direct (H1/title/meta)

Rationale: this exact phrasing (or the tight variant "LLP Registration in India/Online") appears verbatim in the titles of 9 of the ~21 top-ranking pages surveyed (cleartax.in, tallysolutions.com, razorpay.com, registerkaro.in, vakilsearch.com, startupwala.com, twotax.in, finguruindia.com, indiafilings.com), and the SERP researcher explicitly states "Transactional/commercial dominates the 'LLP registration India' query." This is the strongest, most evidenced head term in the dataset — stronger than the bare "LLP in India" phrasing, which appears in only 2 titles (ssrana.in, and implicitly bajajfinserv.in's definitional framing). This mirrors AU Corporate's own established convention on the analogous pillar page: `company-formation`'s primary keyword is "company registration in India," not "companies in India," even though that page is advisory content, not a bare registration platform (per `topics.csv`). The same logic applies here.

Note this is a deliberate divergence from the three sibling entity-type pages' naming pattern (branch/liaison/project office all use bare "X office in India" as primary keyword). That pattern fit those pages because their SERP was RBI/FEMA-establishment content where no comparable "registration"-branded commercial cluster dominated. LLP's SERP is different — it is an MCA-incorporation topic competing directly against registration-platform title conventions, so the primary keyword should reflect that, not force-fit the sibling pattern.

## Foreign/International Framing Check
**Evidence found — limited and specific, not blanket.** The SERP research provides two concrete evidence points that justify foreign/international framing, but only for a bounded sub-topic (FDI eligibility / foreign-partner eligibility), not for the page's primary or entity keyword:
1. A PAA-candidate question, evidenced by direct-answer behavior: *"Can a foreigner be a partner in an LLP in India?"*
2. A real, recurring competitor content cluster specifically targeting this angle: quickcompany.in's "Foreign Direct Investment in LLP for NRI and Foreign Nationals," vjmglobal.com's "Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) in Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)," and the two-part FDI automatic-route gate (100% automatic route only where the sector itself permits it AND there are no FDI-linked performance conditions) stated consistently across IndiaFilings, VJM Global, Academy Tax4Wealth, and QuickCompany.

**Scope of this evidence:** it supports targeting "FDI in LLP" / "foreign investment in LLP in India" / "can a foreigner be a partner in an LLP" as a **directly targeted secondary section and FAQ entry**, addressed to the audience AU Corporate actually serves. It does **not** support inserting "foreign" into the primary keyword ("LLP registration in India" stays as-is) or into the bare entity keyword ("LLP in India" stays as-is) — no evidence surfaced of people searching "foreign LLP registration India" or similar as a head term; the dominant competitor titles are all plain "LLP Registration in India," not internationally-qualified phrasing. Keep the audience/query separation visible: AU Corporate's buyer is disproportionately a foreign investor, but the query they type is still the plain-language one.
