## Claims That Could Not Be Verified — MUST FIX

| Claim | Issue |
|---|---|
| **"Section 115BAA" cited without updating to the Income-tax Act, 2025's renumbering.** The draft states "Most SaaS/AI subsidiaries elect into the concessional corporate tax regime under Section 115BAA" (line 83) and repeats "Section 115BAA" in the FAQ (line 145). | Independently confirmed via multiple sources that Section 115BAA of the 1961 Act was renumbered to **Section 200** in the Income-tax Act, 2025, which is now the operative Act (effective 1 April 2026, i.e., already in force as of today's publish date). This is the exact same class of problem the draft correctly avoided for the SEP citation (using "Section 9(9), Income-tax Act, 2025" instead of the old 1961 framing) — but the draft did not apply the same treatment to the 115BAA citation. As currently written, the page cites a superseded section number for one provision while correctly using the current Act for another, on the same page. **Recommend fixing to**: "Section 200 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (the provision previously numbered Section 115BAA under the 1961 Act)" — both to be accurate and so a reader doesn't reasonably wonder why one citation was updated for the new Act and the other wasn't. Note this same fix is arguably needed on the live `/services/taxation-regulatory` page, which also still says bare "Section 115BAA" in its intro, Quick Facts stat, and FAQ — flagging for consistency but that page is out of scope for this pipeline to edit directly. |
| **GSTR-5A → GSTR-1/GSTR-3B "transition" framing** (lines 95, 107): "the OIDAR-specific filing obligation (GSTR-5A) is replaced by the entity's regular GSTR-1/GSTR-3B filing cadence once the transition is complete... it doesn't just vanish the day your Certificate of Incorporation is issued... your India-billing activity now runs through the new entity's ordinary GST registration." | Could not find an authoritative primary source (CBIC circular, CGST Rules provision) describing this specific transition scenario (a foreign OIDAR/NRTP registration held by the *foreign parent* being wound down once a *separate, newly incorporated Indian subsidiary* takes over billing). What's independently confirmable is that the foreign parent's OIDAR/NRTP registration and the new Indian company's GST registration are two **separate registrations under two separate legal persons** (different PAN/GSTIN) — not one filing that automatically "folds into" or "transitions" into the other. Secondary sources indicate NRTP registrations are formally surrendered via Form GST REG-16 "after completion of business," which suggests an affirmative cancellation step is needed on the old registration, not an automatic changeover. The draft's own earlier paragraph (line 93) correctly warns readers not to assume "your pre-incorporation OIDAR registration and your post-incorporation domestic GST position are the same filing continuing under a new name" — but the later "folds into"/"replaced by" language in lines 95 and 107 sits in some tension with that warning and could still leave a reader assuming continuity rather than two registrations with an affirmative cancellation step on the old one. **Recommend**: either soften to something like "your OIDAR registration needs to be formally wound down (via its own cancellation process) once the new entity's GST registration is in place — the two don't merge automatically," or have a qualified professional confirm the precise mechanics before this section publishes as-is. |

## Regulatory Currency Check

- **Income-tax Act, 2025 is now in force** (effective 1 April 2026, applicable to "Tax Year 2026-27"). As of today (15 Sept 2026), this is the *current* law, not an upcoming one — so Page B is right to cite it for SEP, and needs to apply the same currency standard to the 115BAA→Section 200 citation (see Must Fix above). The live `/services/taxation-regulatory` and `/india-business-setup/company-formation` pages carry the same residual "Section 115BAA" wording and would benefit from the identical fix, though editing those pages is outside this pipeline's scope.
- **No outdated process identified** in FC-GPR/FC-TRS/FLA mechanics, GST domestic thresholds, or the WOS/Branch/Liaison/Project Office/JV entity landscape — all match current practice as independently checked.
- **OIDAR-to-domestic-GST transition mechanics** are the one area where the draft describes a conceptually reasonable but procedurally under-specified process (see Must Fix above) — this is exactly the kind of area the task flagged as highest-risk for misleading-by-omission, and it's the one place I'd stop short of calling "verified."
- **Cross-page consistency with Page A**: Page A does not restate the OIDAR→domestic-GST transition in contradictory terms — it uses matching language ("folds into the new entity's ordinary GST position," "doesn't simply vanish... folds into") consistent with Page B's own phrasing. Both pages share the same underspecified-mechanics issue identified above; a fix should be applied to both for consistency, not just Page B. Page A does not mention equalisation levy dates on Page B, and Page B does not restate them either (confirmed deliberate, per Page B's own writer's notes) — no cross-page date conflict exists because Page B simply doesn't repeat the dates.
- **Page A's Form GST REG-09 citation** (flagged separately by Page A's own fact-checker) appears, based on independent search during this review, to more likely be **Form GST REG-10** for an OIDAR-specific NRTP registration under Rule 14 of the CGST Rules, with REG-09 being the general non-resident taxable person form. This doesn't affect Page B directly (Page B cites no form number), but is worth flagging to the pipeline orchestrator in case Page A's parallel fact-check missed it, since an incorrect form number would be exactly the kind of fabricated-looking specific citation this pipeline is designed to catch.

## Overall Fact-Check Verdict

**Needs writer revision, then human professional sign-off before publish.**

Two concrete fixes are needed before this page is publish-ready:
1. Update the "Section 115BAA" citation (lines 83 and 145) to "Section 200 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (previously Section 115BAA under the 1961 Act)" for consistency with how the page already treats the SEP citation and with the fact that the new Act is now in force.
2. Tighten the GSTR-5A → GSTR-1/GSTR-3B transition language (lines 95, 107) so it doesn't imply one registration automatically converting into another, given that the foreign parent's OIDAR/NRTP registration and the new Indian entity's GST registration are legally separate registrations under separate persons — recommend explicit professional confirmation of the cancellation/surrender mechanics before this section is finalized.

Beyond those two fixes, the remaining flagged items (effective tax rate framing, SEP thresholds, FC-GPR timing, SPICe+ timelines, FDI sector-edge classification) are correctly hedged in the current draft and don't require rewriting, but are time-sensitive/judgment-dependent enough that a qualified AU Corporate professional should sign off on them at or near actual publish time rather than treating this fact-check as a permanent clearance.
