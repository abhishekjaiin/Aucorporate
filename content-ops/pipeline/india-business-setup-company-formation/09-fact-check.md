Scope note on methodology: Primary-source government domains (mca.gov.in, rbi.org.in, indiacode.nic.in bitstream PDFs, indiankanoon.org, incometaxindia.gov.in, and most law-firm/secondary domains such as taxguru.in, vakilsearch.com, ibclaw.in, ca2013.com) were **not reachable** through the network egress proxy during this check (blocked at the proxy layer, not a content issue). Findings below are therefore based on WebSearch result snippets that quote or closely paraphrase the underlying statutory/regulatory text, cross-corroborated across multiple independent, unrelated secondary sources (law firms, tax-compliance platforms, professional bodies) rather than a single source. Where corroboration was strong and consistent across independent sources, a claim is marked Verified; where sourcing was thin, inconsistent, or physically unreachable, it is flagged for human professional verification instead. This is a stricter bar than a normal fact-check and is intentional given the stakes of this content area.

## Regulatory Currency Check

- **Resident-director day count (Section 149(3))**: The draft originally used the pre-2018 "calendar year" framing, which is superseded law. This has been corrected to "financial year" in this pass — this was the single most consequential outdated-process risk identified in this draft, since a foreign parent following the old calendar-year framing could genuinely miscalculate a resident director's compliance window.
- **MCA21 portal version**: As of the draft's own "Last updated: 25 August 2026" date, the legacy V2 portal has already been fully decommissioned (30 June 2026). The writer's original hedge (correctly cautious at drafting time) is now resolved — V3 is not just "current," it is the *only* portal, and omitting this would arguably have been materially incomplete for a page published after the cutover. Now stated explicitly in the body text.
- **OPC eligibility**: The draft's original "available only to Indian residents" description reflects the pre-2021 rule. Since the Companies (Incorporation) Second Amendment Rules, 2021, eligibility turns on Indian **citizenship** (resident or NRI, the latter subject to a 120-day test), not residency alone. Corrected in this pass. This also resolves part of the writer's flagged "120-day" confusion from 04-content-gap.md — the 120-day figure is real, but it is the NRI-eligibility test for OPC formation, not the (182-day, financial-year) resident-director test for ordinary companies. These are two distinct, unrelated thresholds, and the draft was correct not to conflate them.
- **Minimum paid-up capital**: Correctly reflects the post-2015 position (no statutory minimum). No outdated-process risk here.
- **GST thresholds**: Correctly reflects the post-2019 position (Rs 40 lakh for goods, up from the original Rs 20 lakh). No outdated-process risk, but see human-verification note above given thresholds are notification-driven.
- **Income Tax Act 1961 vs. 2025**: The draft correctly avoids committing to a stale Section 90 citation given the live renumbering, consistent with the sitewide approach adopted on the UK cluster page today. No fix needed — the hedge is the correct choice here, not a gap.
