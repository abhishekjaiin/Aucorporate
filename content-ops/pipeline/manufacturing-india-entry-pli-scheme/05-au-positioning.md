## AU Corporate's Right-to-Win Angle
Two things, both grounded in what's actually true of AU's practice, not branding:
1. **The tax-stack connection nobody else makes**: PLI is a disbursed subsidy tied to incremental sales/investment; the 15% concessional manufacturing rate is a separate corporate-tax election. Every competitor examined in Stage 3/4 treats PLI in isolation. AU's own site already documents the concessional rate independently — connecting the two for a manufacturing reader is a genuine, low-effort, high-value first-mover position, not an invented differentiator.
2. **Genuine cross-practice depth already live on the site, correctly cross-linked rather than restated**: AU already has real, specific, dated content on Section 482/Form 5471/Schedule M reconciliation, FC-GPR/FC-TRS/FLA/ECB mechanics, and Safe Harbour rules — deeper than anything found in Stage 3's competitor teardown. The right-to-win move for this new page is not to write that content a second time; it's to be the first page anywhere (competitor or AU's own site) that connects China+1 rationale → PLI mechanics → manufacturing-specific entity/tax/FEMA deltas → pointers into that existing depth, in one reading path. That's Stage 4's central differentiation finding, and AU is structurally positioned to deliver it because the underlying expertise already exists in the cluster — this page's job is synthesis and the manufacturing-specific delta, not first-time coverage of ground already own on the site.

One item needs a factual check before publishing, not an assumption: Stage 3/4's Differentiation item 4 names "Form 5472" alongside Form 5471. Form 5471 is correctly triggered for a US parent's ownership of a foreign (Indian) subsidiary. Form 5472 typically runs the other direction — a 25%-foreign-owned US corporation or a foreign corporation engaged in US trade or business reporting related-party transactions. It is not obviously triggered by a US parent → Indian manufacturing subsidiary fact pattern unless there's a US-side entity that is itself foreign-owned. **Do not assert Form 5472 applies here just because Stage 3/4's gap list named it** — verify against the actual structure being described, or drop it, rather than repeating an unverified competitor-adjacent claim.
