## Recurring Entities & Regulations
- **FEMA 22(R)/2016-RB** — Foreign Exchange Management (Establishment in India of a Branch Office or a Liaison Office or a Project Office or Any Other Place of Business) Regulations, 2016 — cited as the current operative regulation across many sources. Consistent with the Branch Office pipeline finding.
- **RBI draft "Foreign Exchange Management (Establishment in India of a Branch or Office) Regulations, 2025"** — actively discussed by EY, Lexology, Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan, Vinod Kothari Consultants, TaxGuru, Sarafpartners, India Briefing, Stratrich (Oct 2025 draft, comment deadline October 24, 2025 via RBI's "Connect 2 Regulate" portal). Per multiple secondary sources, the draft proposes: (a) a new umbrella category "Entity Resident Outside India (EROI)," (b) consolidation of Branch/Liaison/Project Office into two categories ("Branch" and "Office"), (c) **removal of minimum net worth and profit-track-record eligibility thresholds** for both BO and LO, (d) **elimination of the Liaison Office tenure/validity limit** (currently 3 years / 2 years for NBFC & construction-development entities), and (e) delegation of more approvals to AD banks. **As of 2026-09-21, consistent with the Branch Office pipeline's finding, this remains a draft — not notified/in force.** This is directly relevant to the LO page because the fixed validity/renewal cycle is one of the few features that meaningfully distinguishes LO from BO today, and that distinction is specifically flagged for removal in the draft.
- **Form FNC** (Annex B / Annex-1 of FED Master Direction No. 10/2015-16) — the application form filed with the AD Category-I bank for both BO and LO establishment.
- **RBI / AD Category-I Bank** — approval authority; application is routed through an authorized dealer bank to RBI's Foreign Exchange Department, Mumbai.
- **MCA / Form FC-1** — post-RBI-approval registration with the Registrar of Companies within 30 days, per multiple sources (not RBI-primary-verified in this pass).
- **UIN (Unique Identification Number)** — allocated on RBI approval.
- **Form FC-2 / Section 380(3), Companies Act 2013** — filed with ROC to intimate cessation on closure.
- **AAC (Annual Activity Certificate)** — filed annually with the AD bank and Directorate General of Income Tax (DGIT), New Delhi, by September 30 for the FY ending March 31; failure to file for three consecutive years reportedly triggers an automatic closure process (per Beacon Filing and Treelife summaries — not independently RBI-verified in this pass).
- **Net worth USD 50,000 + 3-year profit track record** for LO, vs. **net worth USD 100,000 + 5-year profit track record** for BO — this is the consistent majority position across sources (one outlier source, a search-engine-synthesized summary citing "vpsandassociates.com," stated the figures inverted/higher; treated as likely an error and NOT used, since the 50k-LO/100k-BO split is both the majority-sourced figure and consistent with the already-confirmed Branch Office pipeline data).
- **Government route vs. RBI/automatic route** — sectors requiring Government-route approval (in consultation with GoI) for LO/BO: defence, telecom, private security, information & broadcasting; also special handling for applicants from Pakistan, Bangladesh, China, Hong Kong, Macau, or for offices proposed in Jammu & Kashmir, North East India, Andaman & Nicobar Islands (police/MHA registration requirements). Nepal-based entities are stated to be permitted to establish only Liaison Offices (not Branch Offices) — a genuinely LO-specific nuance not seen in Branch Office research.
- **Permanent Establishment (PE) / Section 9(1)(i), Income-tax Act 1961 / DTAA Article 5** — recurring compliance theme specific to LO: risk that an LO's actual on-ground activity (e.g., negotiating terms, following up on orders) crosses into PE territory and triggers India taxability, distinct from the BO's already-taxable status. Cited case-law reference: a Supreme Court ruling on LO/PE exposure (per TaxGuru), and separate commentary that LOs and Project Offices "do not constitute a PE" when activities stay within the permitted preparatory/auxiliary scope (per S.S. Rana & Co.).
- **GST** — generally not required for a compliant LO (since it doesn't supply services/goods), but registration becomes necessary if the LO's actual activity strays into taxable-supply territory — same "form vs. substance" theme as the PE risk point above.
