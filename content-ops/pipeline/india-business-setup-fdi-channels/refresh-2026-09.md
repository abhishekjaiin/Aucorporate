## New Authoritative Information Found
All verified against primary/near-primary sources before being added:
1. **Insurance FDI liberalised to 100% Automatic Route** — DPIIT Press Note 1 (2026 Series), issued 9 February 2026, amending para 5.2.22 of the Consolidated FDI Policy following the Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha (Amendment of Insurance Laws) Act, 2025. 100% FDI now Automatic Route for insurance companies and intermediaries; LIC capped at 20% Automatic Route; governance condition requiring a resident-Indian chairperson/MD/CEO. Source: DPIIT PDF (dpiit.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/...), corroborated by Business Standard, Insurance Business Asia, BusinessToday. **This directly contradicted the live page**, which still said "Requires government approval; Max 49% foreign equity."
2. **Revised DPIIT SOP for Government Route processing**, issued May 2026 — fully paperless FIFP/NSWS filing, overall disposal timeline formally extended from 10 to 12 weeks, MHA security-clearance step for sensitive/land-border cases. Source: Lexology, Mondaq legal updates on the SOP (cross-referenced across multiple law-firm client alerts).
3. **Press Note 3 (2020 Series) land-border-country rule, amended March 2026** — Union Cabinet approved a 10%-Chinese-beneficial-ownership carve-out onto the Automatic Route and a 60-day expedited track for seven manufacturing sub-sectors; direct investment from China/Hong Kong-registered entities still requires full Government Route approval. Source: Carnegie Endowment analysis, Lexology, Legal500 client note — cross-checked across three independent write-ups for consistency before use.
4. **E-commerce inventory-model relaxation for exports** — DPIIT Press Note 3 (2026 Series) permits FDI-funded e-commerce entities to use an inventory model exclusively for exporting India-manufactured/produced goods (still prohibited for domestic B2C sales). Source: Medianama, Mondaq, cross-referenced against a DPIIT PDF.
5. **Defence FDI cap raised to 74% Automatic Route** (from 49%) for companies with a new industrial licence, beyond which Government Route approval applies subject to access-to-modern-technology criteria — confirmed as current policy but was previously entirely absent from the page's sector table.
6. **Telecom and private-banking caps corrected**: telecom is 100% overall (49% automatic / balance government route), not "74% in operating entities" as the page stated; private banks are up to 74% total (49% automatic / 49-74% government route), replacing the page's confusing and unsourced "5% automatic, now 20% with approval" line.
7. **Multi-brand retail figures re-verified** ($100M minimum investment, 51% cap, Government Route, 50% of investment into backend infrastructure within 3 years) — the page's existing $100M figure was accurate and preserved, with the backend-infrastructure condition added for completeness.
