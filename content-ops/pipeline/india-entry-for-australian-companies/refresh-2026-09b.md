## New Authoritative Information Found
All new facts added this pass were verified against primary or clearly-attributed secondary sources before use:
- **Australia-India trade figures**: Two-way trade in goods and services ~A$50 billion in 2025; agricultural exports up 131% since 2022 (to ~A$2.3B) and industrial exports excl. coal up 70% since 2022 (to ~A$10.2B); education services exports ~A$9.4 billion — sourced from DFAT's India country brief and corroborating trade-ministry releases.
- **AIBX / South Asia Business Exchange**: Austrade's Australia-India Business Exchange (AIBX) has been repositioned as the India-specific stream of the broader South Asia Business Exchange (SABX, alongside Bangladesh and Sri Lanka streams); the Australian Government committed a further $14.4 million over four years to expand AIBX — confirmed directly on austrade.gov.au's AIBX/SABX program pages.
- **Australia-India Innovation Network (AIIN)**: confirmed as a live Austrade-hosted program (under the AIBX/SABX umbrella) focused on Australian tech-sector access to India's digital economy — sourced directly from austrade.gov.au.
- **Centre for Australia-India Relations (CAIR)**: established 2023 by the Australian Government (announced by the Foreign Minister), runs the Maitri Grants and Fellowships program targeting businesses/SMEs without established India market-access channels — confirmed via foreignminister.gov.au and australiaindiacentre.org.au.
- **India-Australia DTAA Service PE threshold**: confirmed via multiple independent sources that Article 5's services-furnished-through-personnel test uses a 183-day-in-any-12-month-period threshold — notably more generous than the 90-day threshold under the India-US DTAA. This is new content (not previously on the page) and is used to differentiate the Australia hub's PE messaging from the German hub's (which cites the general Article 5 fixed-office test without a specific day count).
- **Australia's own thin capitalisation rules (Division 820, ITAA 1997)**: confirmed the 2023 reform replaced the prior 60%-of-assets safe harbour test with an earnings-based fixed ratio test (30% of tax EBITDA), with the fixed-ratio subdivision's start date delayed to income years starting on or after 1 July 2024 — sourced from ATO guidance and multiple Big-4/law-firm technical alerts (EY, MinterEllison, BDO). Used to make the new Transfer Pricing & Thin Capitalisation card two-sided (India's rule + Australia's own rule), which none of the sibling hubs currently do — a genuine Australia-specific addition rather than a copy of Japan's transfer-pricing card.
- **India's transfer pricing / thin-cap provisions** (Section 92 → Section 161 under Income-tax Act 2025; Section 94B interest-disallowance rule, ₹1 crore threshold, 30% EBITDA cap): reused the same wording already fact-checked and published on the Japan hub this session rather than re-deriving it, since it's a jurisdiction-wide Indian tax rule, not Japan-specific.
- **India-Australia DTAA rates (15% dividends/interest, 10%/15% split royalty-FTS rate) and the Finance Act 2023 domestic-rate correction**: re-verified as still accurate; **not changed** — this was already corrected in the prior pass (`refresh-2026-09.md`) and is restated (not re-derived) in the new FAQ section for consistency.
