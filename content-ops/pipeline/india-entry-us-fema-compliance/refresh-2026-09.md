## New Authoritative Information Found
- **FC-TRS filing window**: confirmed as 60 days from transfer/receipt of consideration (whichever is earlier), filed via the FIRMS portal — not previously stated on the page.
- **FLA Return deadline**: confirmed as 15 July annually (for the FDI/ODI position as of 31 March), consistent across multiple sources — not previously stated on the page (it just said "annually").
- **RBI compounding framework**: governed by the Foreign Exchange (Compounding Proceedings) Rules, 2024 (replacing the 2000 rules), under Section 13 of FEMA, 1999. Compounding amount capped at 300% of the sum involved in the contravention per RBI's guidance note; RBI required to pass its order within 180 days of a complete application. Could not fetch RBI's own Master Direction directly (rbi.org.in is blocked by the environment's egress proxy), so this is sourced from converging secondary summaries (Lexology, compliancecalendar.in via search synthesis, taxguru.in via search synthesis) rather than the primary text — stated on the page with a hedge ("treat as the governing framework rather than a quote... confirm the current amount against RBI's published guidance at the time of filing") rather than as an unhedged hard number, consistent with how the site already handles the Form 3CEB→Form 48 and 182-day resident-director precedents.
- **ECB framework for parent lending**: a foreign equity holder with ≥25% direct equity (or ≥51% indirect, or group-company status) is a "recognised lender" under the ECB rules; loans require Form ECB (Loan Registration Number before first drawdown) and ongoing Form ECB-2 reporting; general Minimum Average Maturity Period of 3 years; framework liberalised 16 February 2026 under the Foreign Exchange Management (Borrowing and Lending) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2026. This is a genuinely new sub-topic for the page, not previously covered anywhere in the US cluster.
- **Form 5471 / FBAR mechanics**: verified Form 5471 Category 5 filer status (10%+ US shareholder of a Controlled Foreign Corporation) and the FBAR "financial interest" rule (>50% ownership of the account-holding entity triggers reporting, $10,000 aggregate threshold, filed with FinCEN/Treasury, separate from Form 5471/IRS). Kept consistent with — and cross-linked to — the existing Form 5471/Schedule M content already live on `transfer-pricing-us-india-subsidiary/page.tsx`, without duplicating or contradicting it.
