## Recurring Entities & Regulations
These appear repeatedly and consistently across independently-published competitor articles, so they represent genuine topical requirements, not AU Corporate's framing:
- **RBI (Reserve Bank of India)** — approval authority for branch offices, via an AD (Authorized Dealer) Category-I bank
- **FEMA, 1999** and specifically **FEMA 22(R)/2016 (Regulation 4)** — the regulation governing branch/liaison/project office establishment
- **RBI's 2025 draft Establishment Rules / 2026 reforms** — a live regulatory development: draft rules reportedly consolidate office types into a new "Entity Resident Outside India (EROI)" category with two buckets, "Branch" and "Office," and reportedly drop the old $100,000 (branch) / $50,000 (liaison) minimum net-worth thresholds. Multiple competitor pages (india-briefing.com, setindiabiz.com) are already covering this.
- **Press Note 2 (2026 series), dated 15 March 2026** — referenced by setindiabiz as an FDI policy update
- **Companies Act, 2013** and **Registrar of Companies (RoC)/MCA** — subsidiary incorporation route
- **UIN (Unique Identification Number)** — issued to approved branch/liaison offices
- **GST, TDS, PF, ESI returns, statutory audit** — cited as compliance obligations common to both structures
- **Dividend/withholding tax (20%, or DTAA-reduced rate)** — repatriation route for subsidiaries vs. free profit repatriation (post-tax) for branch offices
- **Section 115BAB** — concessional 15% (~17.16% effective) corporate tax rate for new manufacturing subsidiaries, cited as a subsidiary-specific advantage
- **Effective tax rates cited repeatedly:** branch office ~40–43.68% (40% base + surcharge + 4% cess) vs. subsidiary ~25.17–29.12% (or 22% base/15% for new manufacturing under 115BAB)
- **DTAA (Double Tax Avoidance Agreement)** — mentioned as relevant to repatriation/withholding, reinforcing why AU Corporate's country-specific (UK/US/Australia) versions exist separately from this generic page
- **Letter of Comfort** — RBI mechanism allowing a subsidiary-of-a-subsidiary applicant to qualify via parent-company eligibility
- **Liaison Office / Project Office** — consistently discussed alongside branch/subsidiary as the fuller entry-structure set; several top-ranking pages frame this as a 3-or-4-way comparison rather than strictly 2-way

## Search Intent (page-level read)
Predominantly **informational-with-commercial-undertone**, not transactional. Every direct-competitor page identified is a blog/guide article (not a pricing or "buy now" page) published by a firm that sells company registration, compliance, or advisory services — classic top-of-funnel content marketing. Evidence: setindiabiz's own social promotion of this exact article pairs it with a "Consult Experts" link; titles favor explainer framing ("Key Differences Explained," "Which Is Best," "Quick Comparison") over service-page framing ("India Subsidiary Registration Services"). No page observed in this research functions as a checkout/quote-request page ranking directly for the comparison query — transactional intent is served by *separate* service pages on the same domains (e.g., setindiabiz.com/branch-office-registration-india, setindiabiz.com/indian-subsidiary-registration), not by the comparison content itself.
