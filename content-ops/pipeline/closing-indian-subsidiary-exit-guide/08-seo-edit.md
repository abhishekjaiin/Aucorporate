## Edits Made

1. **Heading hierarchy fix** — Changed `### Is This the Right Guide for You?` to `## Is This the Right Guide for You?`. As written, the page skipped from H1 straight to H3 before its first true H2, which is an invalid/confusing document outline. Making it H2 keeps the callout as a real, required section (consistent with Stage 5's "not optional" instruction) while fixing the hierarchy.
2. **Primary-keyword H2 insertion** — Changed the first content H2 from "Strike-Off vs Voluntary Liquidation — How to Actually Decide" to "Strike-Off vs Voluntary Liquidation: How to Decide When Closing an Indian Subsidiary." No H2 on the page previously contained the primary keyword phrase. Flagging this as a deviation from Stage 6's exact heading text for visibility — it's a wording-only change, same section/position/content, made to satisfy the primary-keyword-in-H2 checklist item without touching approved structure.
3. **Intro keyword variant** — Changed "If you're a US parent closing an Indian subsidiary..." to "If you're a US parent closing — or winding up — an Indian subsidiary..." to naturally surface the equally-weighted primary keyword variant ("winding up Indian subsidiary," per STATUS.md and Stage 6) that was otherwise absent from the page verbatim. Minimal, doesn't read as inserted.
4. **CTA anchor text fix** — Changed the raw-URL anchor `[/services/taxation-regulatory](/services/taxation-regulatory)` to `[Taxation & Regulatory Services](/services/taxation-regulatory)`. A bare URL as link text is poor practice regardless of placement (CTA-level or not).
5. **Removed spurious "CTA" H2** — Changed `## CTA` to a non-heading label (`**CTA section**`) at the foot of the page. Stage 6's architecture explicitly specifies "no H2 label needed" for this block (unlabeled LeadForm component, matching the sibling pattern); the draft's literal `## CTA` would have shipped an unintended, non-descriptive H2 into the page outline.

No other edits made. The draft otherwise reads naturally, hits the checklist, and closely follows the approved architecture — over-optimizing it further would work against the guide's genuinely useful, non-SEO-copy tone.
