## Search Intent Assessment
**Shifted, but the page was already positioned correctly for it — the fix was depth, not direction.** This query cluster has moved from purely informational to commercially contested (multiple incorporation-service providers publishing detailed pricing/timeline pages to capture the exact query). The page already carried a commercial CTA and a "why no flat fee" position, which is the right call for AU Corporate's advisory model — so no rewrite of the page's angle or positioning was justified. What was missing was the substance a commercially-contested page needs to actually compete: real (if ranged) numbers, a real phase-by-phase timeline, and direct answers to the specific questions searchers ask, plus the one differentiated angle (US-side apostille cost and US tax filing exposure) that no competitor on the SERP currently covers.
