## New Authoritative Information Found
- **DPIIT Press Note No. 1 (2026 Series)** — following Parliament's passage of the Insurance Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025, insurance companies and insurance intermediaries were moved from a 74% FDI cap to **100% FDI under the Automatic Route**, effective February 2026, conditional on at least one of the chairperson/MD/CEO being a resident Indian citizen (LIC remains separately capped at 20% automatic). Corroborated independently across Business Standard, BusinessToday, Insurance Business Magazine, and Angel One reporting, all citing the same Press Note number and legislative basis; a link to the DPIIT-hosted PDF of the notification was also located via search. **Limitation**: direct WebFetch access to the primary DPIIT/PIB source was blocked by the network egress proxy in this environment (both `dpiit.gov.in` and `pib.gov.in` returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED`), so verification here rests on multiple independent, reputable secondary sources reporting consistent, specific detail (Press Note number, effective date, legislative trigger, and the resident-officer condition) rather than a directly-fetched primary document. Added to the page as a concrete, dated illustration that sectoral FDI thresholds shift and should be re-confirmed at planning time — not stated as a general "rates change" platitude.
- **Press Note 3 (2020) — land-border-country FDI approval requirement**: investigated reports of a March 2026 Cabinet-approved easing (a 10% non-controlling automatic-route threshold for land-border-country investors). Found materially **conflicting** secondary reporting — several outlets (Lexology, Vistra, Carnegie Endowment) describe a Cabinet approval, while at least one (Deccan Herald) reports "no changes... sources." Could not reach a primary source to resolve the conflict (egress-blocked). **Not added to the page** — per the guardrail against asserting unverified/contested regulatory claims. This is worth a follow-up check once primary-source access is available, since if confirmed it would be directly relevant to the FDI eligibility and JV cards on this page.
