## Minimum Coverage List
(what the page MUST address to be competitive — table stakes; absence would make the page look thinner than the norm)

1. Clear definitions: branch office = no separate legal personality, extension of the foreign parent, fully liable; subsidiary = separate Indian legal entity incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with limited liability.
2. Permitted vs. prohibited activities, itemized (not just "different activities apply") — export/import trade, consultancy, R&D, IT/software services, buying/selling agency, parent technical support for branch; explicit manufacturing prohibition for branch; unrestricted commercial activity for subsidiary.
3. Regulatory approval route named specifically — RBI approval via an AD Category-I bank under FEMA (current governing regulation: FEMA 22(R)/2016, Regulation 4) for branch offices; RoC/MCA incorporation under the Companies Act, 2013 for subsidiaries.
4. A basic, specific tax-rate comparison (not relative language) — branch ~40–43.68% effective vs. subsidiary ~25.17–29.12% (or 22%/15% under Section 115BAB for new manufacturing), each tied to a section reference or assessment year rather than a bare rounded figure.
5. Liability and control comparison — branch has no liability shield for the foreign parent; subsidiary is a distinct entity with its own board, though typically still parent-controlled as a wholly-owned entity.
6. A side-by-side comparison table (legal status / permitted activities / approval route / tax / liability / repatriation at a headline level) — the near-universal structural convention across the set; its absence would read as less useful than every ranking competitor.
7. Basic minimum-capital/net-worth requirement figures for branch offices, framed correctly against current FEMA thresholds.
8. A high-level repatriation mention (branch profits generally repatriable post branch-profit-tax without further dividend withholding; subsidiary dividends attract withholding, DTAA-reduced where a treaty applies) — kept intentionally general here, not built out into country-specific DTAA tables (that's what the UK/US/Australia pages already do — see Minimum Coverage note below on scope boundary).

**Scope boundary (per parent brief):** DTAA/repatriation coverage on this page should stop at the country-agnostic mechanism-level explanation above, then route readers with a covered home jurisdiction to `app/india-entry-for-uk-companies/uk-subsidiary-vs-branch-office-india`, `app/india-entry-for-us-companies/us-subsidiary-vs-branch-office-india`, and `app/india-entry-for-australian-companies/australia-subsidiary-vs-branch-office-india` for the deep, verified treaty-specific detail. Do not attempt to match treelife's or setindiabiz's tax specificity by inventing country-specific numbers here — match it at the generic-mechanism level and let the routing layer do the rest. Similarly, do not expand into a full liaison/project-office comparison to match incorpx.io/india-briefing's 3-to-5-way scope — mention those structures exist and link out/cross-reference, per Stage 2's explicit scope guidance, rather than retargeting the page.

## Differentiation List
(what almost nobody covers well, or covers at all — the real opportunity; every item below traces to a specific observed gap in Stage 3)

1. **Accurate, plainly-labeled RBI draft Establishment Regulations / "EROI" status.** Zero of the 6 analyzed pages were confirmed to state this correctly (still draft/not notified, FEMA 22(R)/2016 remains current law); incorpx.io's "(2026)"-dated title creates a freshness expectation it doesn't appear to meet. This is Stage 3's headline finding — closing it alone would beat every page reviewed on this one point.
2. **Sourced, non-conflicting setup-timeline figures.** setindiabiz (15–20 business days subsidiary / 30–45 days branch) and kanakkupillai (4–6 weeks / up to 8 weeks) directly contradict each other, neither cites a source. Attribute timeline claims to an actual processing benchmark (RBI/AD-bank practice, MCA SPICe+ average) or state clearly that timelines vary by AD bank and case.
3. **A real, operationalized decision framework** ("choose branch if... / choose subsidiary if...") going beyond india-briefing's partial version — built around parameters a founder/CFO actually weighs: expected duration of India presence, exit/wind-up ease, appetite for full liability, pre-revenue vs. revenue-generating activity (tying into the compliance-burden point below), sector/activity restrictions, and funding structure. This directly answers the one confirmed-verbatim PAA-style question in the research (the Quora thread title).
4. **Branch-to-subsidiary conversion mechanics as a full section**, not a passing mention — process, re-incorporation route under the Companies Act, 2013, what's retained vs. restarted, realistic cost/time. Only setindiabiz touches this, and only briefly.
5. **Post-setup compliance-burden warning for branch offices used pre-revenue** (Annual Activity Certificate, DGP reporting, Form FC-3) — treelife.in is the only page in the set to raise this, and it's a genuinely practical warning no one else surfaces.
6. **A worked numeric example** (₹ figures, not just percentages) showing branch vs. subsidiary tax outcome on a representative profit figure — only treelife does this; it's the strongest content-quality differentiator Stage 3 identified in the entire set.
7. **Visible author/reviewer credential.** Zero of 6 analyzed pages show a named professional (CA/CS/lawyer) despite all being advisory firms with real expertise — a checkable, cheap E-E-A-T win.
8. **Visible "last updated" date and a stated assessment year for all tax figures** — only treelife confirmed to do the former; matching both closes a trust gap visible across the category.
9. **Outbound citation to primary sources** (RBI FEMA master circular/FAQ, MCA, Invest India) — none of the 6 analyzed pages confirmed to link out, despite Stage 1 confirming these sources exist, are on-topic, and are cited "in spirit" throughout the category but don't rank or get linked to directly. Linking out is a low-cost differentiator nobody in the set is doing.
10. **Minimum-capital/net-worth thresholds framed as a dual state** — current $100K (branch)/$50K (liaison) figures under FEMA 22(R)/2016, explicitly flagged alongside the draft reform's reported removal of those thresholds — nobody in the set frames both states together.
11. **Light-touch mention of the Letter of Comfort mechanism** (RBI route allowing a subsidiary-of-a-subsidiary applicant to qualify via parent-company eligibility) — a real, cited entity from Stage 1's broader research that none of the 6 deep-analyzed pages cover; worth one paragraph/callout, not a full section.
12. **A routing/hub layer instead of scope creep.** No page in the set runs a "your country is covered elsewhere, click here" pattern — competitors either stay generic or expand into 3-to-5-way liaison/project-office comparisons. Explicitly cross-linking to the UK/US/Australia pages for readers whose jurisdiction is covered, while staying generic for everyone else, is a structural approach nothing in the competitor set does and matches this page's actual mandate.

## Unanswered PAA/Related Questions
(from Stage 1's candidate PAA list — none resolved well by the analyzed pages)

- **"Which is better, branch office or subsidiary, in India for a foreign company?"** — no page in the set gives a genuine parameter-weighted answer; india-briefing gets closest with a generic (not foreign-specific) decision framework, but doesn't operationalize it into a usable "if X then Y" structure.
- **"Can a branch office be converted into a subsidiary in India?"** — only setindiabiz addresses it, and only in passing (names the re-incorporation route, no process detail, cost, or timeline).
- **"What is the minimum capital requirement for a branch office in India / RBI approval?"** — coverage across the set is thin/inconsistent, and where present, not confirmed to note that the thresholds are under proposed reform — a real accuracy risk if repeated uncritically.
- **"What are the parameters to be considered by a foreign company while deciding whether to have branch office in India or a wholly owned subsidiary?"** (confirmed-verbatim Quora thread title) — no page in the analyzed set fully operationalizes this into a working decision framework; this is the single clearest unmet-demand signal in the research.
- Adjacent, explicitly out-of-scope-for-this-page question (per Stage 2 guidance): "liaison office vs branch office vs project office vs subsidiary india" — real demand (incorpx.io, india-briefing, ahlawatassociates cover 3-to-5-way versions), but this page should address it only as context/internal link, not by expanding scope.

## Potential Unique Angles
(grounded in the gaps above, not invented)

- Lead with the RBI draft Establishment Regulations/EROI status, stated correctly (draft, not notified, 2016 framework still governs) — closes Stage 3's headline gap and is a genuine freshness/accuracy differentiator against incorpx.io's "(2026)"-dated-but-silent-on-this-point page.
- A "parameters to weigh" decision framework built directly off the confirmed Quora question — duration of India presence, liability appetite, exit ease, activity type (commercial/manufacturing-restricted vs. unrestricted), pre-revenue compliance burden, and funding structure — none of the analyzed pages build this out fully.
- A standalone conversion-mechanics section (branch → subsidiary) — genuinely thin across the set; setindiabiz is the only page to even name the route.
- A compliance-burden callout for branch offices used pre-revenue (AAC/DGP/FC-3), generalized from treelife's unique point — a real, practical warning almost nobody else gives.
- A worked ₹ tax example alongside the rate table (matching treelife's approach, the strongest content-quality differentiator observed) rather than stopping at percentages the way setindiabiz, ebizfiling, and kanakkupillai do.
- A visible author credential and last-updated date — a category-wide miss (0/6 and 1/6 respectively) that's cheap for AU Corporate to close.
- A routing layer (not a content-depth competition) for DTAA/repatriation and for liaison/project-office detail — explicitly send UK/US/Australia readers to AU Corporate's existing deep country pages, and mention liaison/project office only as a pointer, rather than trying to out-depth treelife on tax or out-scope india-briefing on structure count. This plays to what none of the competitor set is set up to do (they're single generic articles with no country-specific sibling pages to route to).
- A minimum-capital table that shows both the current FEMA thresholds and the draft reform's proposed removal side by side, clearly dated — no competitor frames this as a "before/after" rather than picking one state to state as fact.

## Opportunities for Authoritative Sourcing
(where citing MCA/RBI/Income Tax/GST primary sources would beat what's currently ranking)

- **RBI FEMA 22(R)/2016 (Regulation 4)** and the RBI Master Direction/FAQ on Branch/Liaison/Project Offices — cite and link directly for the approval-route description; setindiabiz names the regulation but no page in the set is confirmed to link to it.
- **RBI's draft Establishment Regulations / EROI consultation material** — cite directly with a clear "draft, as of [date], not yet notified" framing; this is the one piece of sourcing that, done correctly, closes Stage 3's single biggest identified gap.
- **Companies Act, 2013 / MCA SPICe+ process** — cite MCA directly for subsidiary incorporation mechanics and timeline instead of repeating setindiabiz's or kanakkupillai's unsourced, conflicting day/week ranges.
- **Income Tax Act, Section 115BAB** (concessional rate for new manufacturing subsidiaries) and the general corporate tax rate/surcharge/cess schedule for branch offices (treated as a foreign company/PE) — cite the Act/CBDT rate schedule directly rather than a rounded percentage, and tag every rate to an assessment year the way only treelife currently does.
- **Press Note 2 (2026 series), 15 March 2026 (DPIIT/FDI policy)** — cite directly if referencing current FDI-route eligibility, rather than paraphrasing secondhand as setindiabiz appears to.
- **Invest India "Doing Business in India" FAQ/investor guide** — Stage 1 found this ranks nowhere organically for the core query despite being on-topic and authoritative; citing/linking it is a genuine, uncontested E-E-A-T opportunity no ranking competitor is using.
- **RBI FAQs on Letter of Comfort / branch-liaison-project office eligibility** — supports the minor Letter of Comfort callout with a real primary-source citation instead of leaving it unsourced or omitted entirely, as all 6 analyzed pages currently do.
