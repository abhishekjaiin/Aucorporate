## Foreign/International Framing Check
Evidence found for international-audience phrasing, and how it's handled:
1. **taxguru.in ranks with the exact title** "Everything about Branch Office/ Project Office/ Liaison Office by Foreign Company in India" (Stage 1, ranking-pages table, row 15) — genuine SERP evidence that "foreign company" framing appears in real ranking content for this cluster. This justifies using "foreign company" naturally in body copy and possibly one supporting long-tail phrase, but does **not** justify inserting "foreign" into the primary keyword or H1 — the dominant title pattern across the other 14 direct-competitor rows in that same table uses plain "branch office in India" / "branch office registration in India" without "foreign," and that plain form is what this page should target directly.
2. **PAA-style question**: "Can a company from Pakistan, China or Bangladesh open a branch office in India?" — genuine evidence of nationality-specific search interest tied to the government-consultation/restricted-country list (Stage 1, Recurring Entities section). This justifies a dedicated eligibility/restrictions subsection addressing specific-country scrutiny, addressed naturally rather than as a targeted keyword phrase (the query is too fragmented across country names to anchor a single keyword on).
3. **"foreign company registration India"** appears in Stage 1's Related Searches cluster (line 58) — real evidence of demand for that broader phrase, but it is already the Company Registration pillar's territory (see Supporting Queries above), not this page's.
4. No evidence anywhere in Stage 1's data (titles, PAA-style questions, related-search cluster, recurring entities) of "international branch office India," "overseas branch office India," or "cross-border branch office India" as real search phrasing — these would be manufactured framing and are excluded entirely.

**Net guidance**: target the plain, broad-market phrasing ("branch office in India," "branch office registration in India," "RBI approval for branch office in India") as the page's direct keyword targets. Address the foreign-investor audience and nationality-specific restrictions through content and structure (who this page is for, restricted-country callout, FEMA cross-border mechanics) — not by inserting "foreign/international/overseas" into the primary or secondary keyword slots.
