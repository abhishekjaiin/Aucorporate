# Search Console Follow-up Review: theaucorp.com — 2026-08-26

## Scope
One-day follow-up to `content-ops/performance/2026-08-sitewide.md` (2026-08-25 site-wide audit), checking the impact of four changes made since that audit:
1. Refresh of `/india-entry-for-uk-companies/uk-subsidiary-vs-branch-office-india`
2. Rebuild of `/india-business-setup/company-formation` into a full pillar page (was 46 lines)
3. Rework of `/doing-business-in-india/incorporation`, `/doing-business-in-india/post-incorporation`, and the `/doing-business-in-india` hub
4. Two sitemap resubmissions

## Tooling note (read before the findings below)
The task asked for `inspect_url_enhanced` / `batch_url_inspection` to pull `last_crawled` timestamps. **Neither tool is available in this session's toolset.** The closest available substitute, `check_indexing_issues`, was used instead — it confirms index status, robots/fetch state, and Google's chosen-vs-declared canonical, but it does **not** return a last-crawled date or direct confirmation that a specific sitemap resubmission was processed. Treat the "indexed" findings below as index-status confirmation, not crawl-freshness confirmation. If last-crawled timestamps are a hard requirement, this needs to be re-run with a tool/session that exposes URL Inspection API data directly.

## 2. Site-wide Ranking Collapse — Recovery Check

**Bottom line: no recovery signal yet, and it is genuinely too early to expect one from changes made ~1 day ago. The site is still sitting in the same degraded, volatile state described in the 2026-08-25 audit.**

Aggregate daily position trend (28 days, 2026-07-29 to 2026-08-26):
- **2026-07-29 to 2026-08-12** (pre-relaunch impact window / early relaunch): position mostly in the 7-20 range (e.g., 7/29: 9.4, 8/3: 7.4, 8/4: 8.9, 8/11: 10.5, 8/12: 14.8)
- **2026-08-13 to 2026-08-25** (post-relaunch, includes the follow-up changes): position swings between 23 and 51.5 with no clear downward (improving) trend line — 8/15: 49.6, 8/17: 35.3, 8/19: 37.1, 8/22: 51.5, 8/24: 34.3, 8/25: 26.7. The last two days available (8/24, 8/25) are not meaningfully better than the mid-month low points (8/15, 8/22) — this reads as noise around a still-elevated baseline, not a recovery curve.
- 14-day total (2026-08-12 to 2026-08-26): 9 clicks, 578 impressions, 1.56% CTR, **avg. position 33.0** — worse than the 28-day blended average of 24.2, confirming the degraded state has not started reversing in the most recent two weeks.

**One useful nuance found this round:** the flagship branded query **"au corporate" has held a stable position of roughly 6-11 (mostly 8-9) every single day across the entire 28-day window**, including through the relaunch and the follow-up changes — it did not collapse. That means the homepage's blended average position drop (8.6 → 17-28 in various cuts) is being driven by the page ranking for a wider spread of new/weaker queries alongside "au corporate," diluting the average — not by the flagship query itself losing rank. This slightly narrows the "site-wide collapse" story: the core brand term is intact; it's the broader page-level average and the previously-ranking non-brand pages that moved. Worth carrying into the next full audit as the more precise framing.

The 5 changed pages specifically:
- `/india-business-setup/company-formation`, `/doing-business-in-india/incorporation`, `/doing-business-in-india/post-incorporation`: **zero impressions in Search Console for the last 28 days** — no query data at all yet. Indexed but not yet showing up in any search result GSC has logged. Expected and not a red flag at ~1 day post-change; these pages simply haven't accumulated enough crawl/serve cycles to register.
- `/doing-business-in-india` (hub): 1 impression total in 28 days, position 88 on "doing business in india" — consistent with the site-wide collapse pattern already documented, no new information from the rework yet.
- `/india-entry-for-uk-companies/uk-subsidiary-vs-branch-office-india`: 28 impressions in 28 days across "branch or subsidiary uk" (20 impr, position 29.9) and "branch vs subsidiary uk" (6 impr, position 33.7). **Position is essentially unchanged from the 2026-08-25 audit (was 29.9 then, is 29.9 now)** — the refresh has not yet moved this query, which is expected this soon but means there is no early positive signal to report either.

**Verdict: too early to tell on all fronts.** Nothing here contradicts the "site relaunch is mid-reprocessing" explanation from the prior audit, and nothing here shows the follow-up changes have started working yet — both are expected outcomes at the 1-day mark. Recommend re-checking in 2-3 weeks (mid-to-late September) once there's been time for re-crawling and re-evaluation.
