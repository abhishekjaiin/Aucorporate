## Relevant Service Lines for This Page

- **Company Incorporation / India Business Setup** — the core of the page. AU Corporate genuinely runs this end to end (SPICe+, MCA filings, entity structuring) — this is table-stakes substantiation, not a stretch.
- **FEMA & RBI Compliance** — directly substantiated on-site already (regulatory-compliance and banking-taxation pages name FC-GPR, FC-TRS, the annual FLA return, and the FIRMS portal with real specificity). This is the backbone of the differentiation opportunity around FDI reporting, FOCC, and the post-incorporation roadmap. Genuinely AU Corporate's strength, not a claimed one.
- **FDI Advisory** — Automatic vs. Government Route is already a live AU Corporate page (india-business-setup/fdi-channels) with sector-specific detail. Belongs on this pillar as a summary with a link out, reinforcing rather than duplicating.
- **GST Advisory & Compliance** — relevant strictly as the threshold-triggered post-incorporation step it actually is (banking-taxation page already states the Rs 20 lakh/Rs 40 lakh thresholds and the voluntary-registration/LUT logic for entities billing an overseas parent). Belongs as one node in the compliance roadmap, not a standalone GST sales pitch.
- **Corporate Tax / International Tax / Transfer Pricing** — relevant for the CFO-level FAQ specifically: DTAA interaction with repatriation, Form 3CEB for international related-party transactions. This is a real, demonstrated depth area (india-entry-for-australian-companies already runs genuine DTAA mechanics — 15% withholding, split royalty rate, the Finance Act 2023 domestic-rate interaction). Scope to the FAQ, not a full corporate-tax-advisory section.
- **Virtual CFO** — the natural anchor for the "CFO-level FAQ" differentiation item (repatriation mechanics, bank-account sequencing, missed-filing consequences). AU Corporate claims this service sitewide (services metadata, schema.org markup, about page) even though there is currently no dedicated /virtual-cfo page — treat it as a real, named service line the firm offers, but don't link to a URL that doesn't exist.
- **Accounting & Outsourcing / Payroll** — relevant only as brief roadmap nodes (statutory books under the Companies Act, PAN/TAN, PF/ESI once headcount thresholds are crossed) — this is already how banking-taxation and regulatory-compliance treat them. Do not expand into a standalone HR/payroll pitch on a registration-intent page.
- **Compliance & Regulatory Services** — the umbrella for the consolidated post-incorporation roadmap (AOC-4/MGT-7 annual filings, statutory audit, AGM). This is where AU Corporate's genuine right-to-win sits: it runs this as an ongoing service, not just the incorporation transaction.

## Service Lines Deliberately Excluded

- **Business Valuation, Transaction Advisory, Arbitration Services** — these are downstream, event-triggered services (M&A, disputes, fundraising) with no natural connection to a reader who is asking "how do I register a company in India." Forcing them in would read as an unrelated cross-sell.
- **Audit & Assurance as a dedicated section** — the statutory-audit obligation belongs as one line item inside the annual-filing roadmap node (which the site already does correctly on regulatory-compliance/banking-taxation). It should not become its own promotional section arguing why AU Corporate's audit practice is superior — that's a different reader's question.
- **Risk Advisory / Internal Controls** — real AU Corporate service lines, but not something a first-time incorporation reader is asking about. Including them here would be scope-creep aimed at selling services rather than answering the query.
- **GCC Setup as a full section** — genuinely connected (a Global Capability Center is one reason a foreign parent incorporates), and it already has its own dedicated page (/gcc-setup-india) plus a mention on the Australia entry page. A single sentence + link is appropriate; a full GCC pitch does not belong on a general registration pillar.

## International-Investor Considerations Relevant Here

Based on Stage 4's minimum-coverage and differentiation lists, and cross-checked against what the live site already states elsewhere so the pillar doesn't contradict it:

- **Indian entity structures** — summary-level only (Pvt Ltd, LLP, OPC, WOS, Branch/Liaison/Project Office, JV), per Stage 4's decision to link out to the dedicated entity-comparison content rather than rebuild the decision framework here.
- **FDI route (Automatic vs. Government)** — needed; summarize and link to /india-business-setup/fdi-channels rather than re-publish its sector table.
- **FEMA/RBI angle** — needed and central to the differentiation opportunity: FC-GPR (30-day window), FC-TRS, the annual FLA return, and — as the genuinely uncovered technical concept — FOCC (Foreign Owned or Controlled Company / downstream investment). This is real FEMA compliance territory AU Corporate's regulatory practice already operates in.
- **Tax treatment** — DTAA/repatriation belongs in the CFO FAQ; a full corporate-tax-planning discussion does not belong on a registration-intent page.
- **GST** — needed as the threshold-triggered post-incorporation step already documented on banking-taxation; not a standalone GST-advisory pitch.
- **Accounting/compliance obligations & post-incorporation requirements** — this is the page's strongest legitimate opportunity (see Right-to-Win below): AOC-4/MGT-7, statutory audit, AGM, FLA, and (where applicable) Form 3CEB, sequenced with real deadlines.
- **Practical India-entry considerations** — bank-account-opening sequencing (already documented with real specificity on banking-taxation: CoI → PAN → MOA/AOA → KYC → apostille of foreign documents → AD Category-I bank → FIRC → FC-GPR), resident-director requirement, apostille/notarization distinction for a foreign director's documents.

Not every item needs full-section treatment here — several (GST detail, FDI sector tables, entity decision framework) are better served as a concise on-page summary plus an internal link to the page that already owns that depth, per Stage 4's own architecture decisions.

## AU Corporate's Right-to-Win Angle

1. **It already runs the post-incorporation compliance function, not just the incorporation transaction.** The regulatory-compliance and banking-taxation pages show AU Corporate can already state, with real specificity, the FC-GPR deadline, the FLA annual obligation, the AOC-4/MGT-7 filing pair, PF/ESI thresholds, and the GST voluntary-registration/LUT logic for entities billing an overseas parent. Competitors in Stage 3/4's analyzed set treat incorporation and ongoing compliance as separate services sold by separate teams (or don't sell the ongoing piece at all); AU Corporate can credibly narrate incorporation as the first step in one continuous compliance relationship, because that is literally how its service lines are structured. This directly satisfies Stage 4 differentiation item #3 (the consolidated roadmap) — the individual facts already exist on AU Corporate's own site, scattered across four separate pages; this pillar's job is to sequence them into one narrative and link out for depth, not re-invent them.
2. **CFO-level FAQ is backed by a real Virtual CFO + Taxation/FEMA practice, not just claimed.** The Australia entry page already demonstrates AU Corporate can go two levels deeper than "check the DTAA" — it states the actual withholding rates, the royalty-rate split, and how the Finance Act 2023 domestic-rate cut changes the repatriation calculus. That is a genuine technical-depth signal Stage 4 confirmed no competitor page has. The pillar should carry this same standard of specificity in its FAQ for the general (non-Australia-specific) reader: repatriation routes, DTAA interaction, bank-account sequencing, and the real consequence of a missed FC-GPR filing (compounding under FEMA, not a vague "penalties may apply").
3. **The Australia-specific entry point is real, not aspirational.** /india-entry-for-australian-companies exists today with genuine ECTA/DTAA/GCC content. This pillar should link to it naturally (e.g., in the FAQ or a "entering from a specific country" callout) rather than compete with it — see cannibalization note below.
4. **Primary-source citation is already AU Corporate's house style, not a new stretch.** The regulatory-compliance and banking-taxation pages already cite specific acts by name (Companies Act 2013, FEMA 1999, Income Tax Act 2025, DPDP Act 2023, Trade Marks Act 1999) rather than vague "as per applicable law" phrasing. The writer should match this existing standard on the new pillar — it's consistent with what's already published, so it reads as house voice, not a one-off SEO tactic.

## Explicit Guidance for the Writer

- **Write as the practitioner who runs this, not as an advert for the practitioner who runs this.** Every fact should be usable regardless of whether the reader ever contacts AU Corporate. Avoid sentences that exist only to assert quality ("industry-leading," "trusted partner," "seamless experience") — the existing site pages you're extending mostly avoid this already (banking-taxation and regulatory-compliance read as technical explainers, not sales copy); match that register, not the promotional tone in a few of the hub/CTA sections (e.g., india-business-setup/page.tsx's "Why Choose AU Corporate" block is closer to the line — do not import that tone into the pillar body).
- **Do not re-litigate figures already published elsewhere on the site.** If you state a timeline or cost range, it must reconcile with the 4-6 week (Automatic Route) / 8-12 week (Government Route) figures on fdi-channels and the 8-12 week total-setup figure on timeline-resources. If those figures themselves need updating, flag it — don't quietly publish a third, different number on this page.
- **Condense, then link — don't duplicate.** For FDI route detail, the six compliance regimes, and bank-account sequencing, write 2-4 sentences that stand on their own and then link to the dedicated page for full depth. The differentiation value here is the roadmap and the sequencing, not a second copy of paragraphs that already exist on banking-taxation/regulatory-compliance.
- **Cite by instrument and section/form number**, matching the house style already on regulatory-compliance/banking-taxation (Companies Act 2013 provisions, FEMA 1999, the specific FC-GPR/FC-TRS/FLA forms, current SPICe+ version). Vague "as per applicable law" phrasing is exactly what Stage 4 found the entire competitive field guilty of — don't reproduce it.
- **Keep the CFO FAQ genuinely technical.** Repatriation routes (dividend, buyback, capital reduction), DTAA interaction (cite Income Tax Act Section 90), bank-account-opening sequence, and the real consequence of a missed FC-GPR filing (compounding under FEMA, not just "penalties apply") — this section is the sharpest differentiation opportunity and the one place where sounding under-specific will cost the most credibility.
- **FOCC section: keep it short and precise, not padded.** It's a genuine, useful, currently-uncovered concept, but it's also easy to get technically wrong or to over-explain. A tight, accurate paragraph beats a long one.
- **Do not fabricate a named-author byline.** The site currently has no individual CA/partner byline pattern (about page describes the team collectively — CAs, CPAs, Company Secretaries, Cost Accountants, Lawyers, Ex-Bankers, MBAs — with no named-individual credential box found anywhere on-site). Stage 4's "credentialed authorship" differentiation item should be delivered as a firm-level credential statement (e.g., "prepared by AU Corporate's taxation and regulatory compliance practice"), not as an invented named author with fabricated credentials.
- **Do not claim or build an interactive cost/timeline estimator or downloadable calendar.** Stage 4 flags this (differentiation item #9) as unclaimed ground, but nothing like it exists on the site today and building one is a product/dev decision outside this content pass's scope. The achievable substitute — and the one to actually deliver — is the honestly reconciled static cost/timeline table (item #1), presented with real confidence since it's consistent with the site's own existing figures. Flag the interactive tool as a deferred/future idea in the internal notes, not as a page feature.
- **Keep the Australia framing to a natural link, not a redirect of the whole page's voice.** Per Stage 2's own keyword finding, "foreign company registration in India" underperforms the plain phrasing — write for the general foreign-investor reader throughout, and surface the Australia angle as one FAQ line or a "if you're entering from a specific market" callout linking to /india-entry-for-australian-companies (and the equivalent US/UK/Singapore/Germany/Japan/China pages), not as a page-wide pivot.
- **Where the honest answer is "no service belongs here," leave it out.** Several sections (documents checklist, procedural skeleton, minimum-capital FAQ) are pure informational table stakes — resist the urge to append a service pitch to every subsection just because AU Corporate offers something adjacent.
