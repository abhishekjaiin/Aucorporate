## New Authoritative Information Found
1. **RBI draft Foreign Exchange Management (Establishment in India of a Branch or Office) Regulations, 2025** — released 3 October 2025 for public consultation, proposing to replace the 2016 branch/liaison/project office framework: removing the USD 100,000 net-worth and 5-year profit-track-record eligibility criteria, and moving from a fixed permitted-activities list to a principle-based approach. Independently re-verified this session via search-result corroboration across TaxGuru, Lexology (two separate write-ups), India Briefing, and Mondaq. As of the most recent information found (stakeholder consultation completed, expected effective FY2026-27, sourced from reporting dated through March 2026), **this remains a draft — not notified in the Official Gazette**. Added as a clearly-labeled, dated "regulatory watch" callout, with the operative table rows above it kept to the rules currently in force.
2. **Finance Act, 2024 cut the foreign-company/branch corporate tax rate from 40% to 35%**, effective FY 2024-25 onward — this figure was already vetted in the UK sibling's refresh via multiple independent tax-advisory sources; re-confirmed here as applicable to any foreign company's branch/PE regardless of the parent's home country, since India's domestic foreign-company tax rate isn't nationality-specific. Not previously on this page at all.
3. **Section 115BAA's ~25.17% effective concessional domestic-company rate** for subsidiaries — same status as in the UK refresh: unchanged in substance, verified via ClearTax/TaxBuddy-class explainers cross-checked in the prior UK session and re-confirmed via this session's SERP research (PwC, Beacon Filing). Added without citing a specific section number, consistent with the UK page's approach, since section numbering is mid-transition under the Income-tax Act, 2025.
4. **USD 100,000 net worth / 5-year profit track record** as the current (still-in-force) RBI branch-office eligibility test — verified via India Briefing, RBI's own published Master Direction/FAQ-style documentation reference, and Setindiabiz. This is standing law, not new, but was a genuine content gap on our page.
5. **IRS Form 926 / IRC Section 6038B** — verified directly via IRS.gov (Form 926 filing requirement page and "About Form 926"): a US person transferring more than $100,000 cash/property to a foreign corporation within a 12-month period, or acquiring ≥10% ownership, must file Form 926. This is a genuinely useful, verifiable, US-specific structural difference between funding a subsidiary (a "transfer to a foreign corporation") and funding a branch (not a separate corporation, so Form 926 doesn't apply the same way) that was not represented anywhere on the page before, despite the page already discussing Form 5471/8858.
6. **India-US DTAA Article 7 "functionally separate entity" profit-attribution test** for branch PEs — verified via search-result summaries citing the treaty text and PE-attribution commentary (Lexology, Sorting Tax, Beacon Filing on PE risk for US companies). Used to explain *why* branch profit attribution is a recurring dispute, not just state the tax rate — kept at a structural/compliance-tradeoff level per instructions, without inventing unverifiable numeric outcomes.
7. **Branch office closure mechanics vs. subsidiary strike-off/voluntary liquidation** — verified via Treelife, Beacon Filing, and ASC Group summaries: branch closure requires RBI approval via an AD Category-I bank (original RBI permission letter, auditor's liability-clearance certificate, ROC closure certificate, Form 15CA/15CB) and typically adds 2-6 months versus a dormant subsidiary's faster Section 248 strike-off route.
