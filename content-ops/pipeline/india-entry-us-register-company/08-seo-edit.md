## Edits Made

1. **Intro paragraph (opening lines under the H1).** Changed "You've decided to register a company in India — this page is about *how*, not *whether*." to "...register a company in India from the USA — this page is about *how*, not *whether*." and inserted one new sentence immediately after it: "In short: you'll choose a structure (Private Limited subsidiary, LLP, Branch, Liaison, or Project Office), file through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs or the RBI depending on which one, and clear India's resident-director and apostille requirements — typically 4-6 weeks to incorporate and 8-12 weeks to be fully operational."
   - Why: closes the primary-keyword-in-first-100-words gap (the original didn't pair "register a company in India" with "from the USA" until well past the 100-word mark) and creates a ~48-word, directly-answering paragraph positioned at the very top of the page — a legitimate featured-snippet candidate for "how to register a company in India from the USA" / "register company in India from USA" style queries. No new facts introduced — every figure in the new sentence (5 structures, MCA vs. RBI split, resident-director/apostille gates, 4-6/8-12 weeks) already appears later on the page and traces to the same sibling-page sources the writer cited. Kept short deliberately so it reads as a genuine summary, not a keyword-stuffed insert.

2. **FAQ section — question formatting.** Converted all 8 FAQ questions from bold paragraph text (`**Question?**`) to H3 headings (`### Question?`), each still immediately followed by its existing direct-answer sentence.
   - Why: FAQPage/FAQ-rich-result eligibility and general accessibility/SEO best practice both call for each question to be a real heading element, not styled text. This was a pure markup fix — no answer copy was reworded, shortened, or reordered.

No other edits were made. The rest of the draft — keyword placement, section order, comparison table, internal links, CTA copy — already met the checklist on review; over-editing it further would have risked exactly the "reads like SEO copy" failure mode this stage is meant to avoid.
