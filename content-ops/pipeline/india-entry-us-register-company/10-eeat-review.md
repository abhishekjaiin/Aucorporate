That kind of self-aware hedge is a stronger trust signal than a confident-sounding but unverifiable claim would have been — and it matches the voice already established on `branch-office-in-india` ("we were not able to confirm... against a primary RBI source") and `llp-in-india` ("hasn't been independently verified against the primary Rules text").

## Editorial Call: RBI October 2025 Draft Reform Pointer

**Decision: add it. Done — see fix #1 above.**

Reasoning:
- **What the siblings actually do.** `branch-office-in-india` and `liaison-office-in-india` both carry a prominent, boxed "Regulatory watch" callout naming the draft regulation, stating plainly it hasn't been gazetted, and telling the reader to plan against current law. `liaison-office-in-india` repeats the point a second time in its Validity/Renewal section and again in an FAQ answer. `project-office-in-india` treats it more lightly — one sentence, inline in body prose, no separate box — because Project Office eligibility doesn't turn on the net-worth thresholds the draft would change. That's 3 of 3 relevant sibling pages, with the treatment scaled to how directly the draft affects that page's own content.
- **Why this isn't "out of scope" for a synthesis page.** Stage 6's architecture explicitly scopes this page as a thin routing layer that states sibling-page facts briefly and links out for depth — that's a real and correct constraint, and it's why I did not rebuild the full regulatory-watch box here. But the constraint is about depth, not about omitting a fact that changes the reliability of a number this page states in its own table. The 5-way comparison table is this page's single most load-bearing, differentiated artifact (per Stage 6's own "Unique Content Angle" framing) — if the exact figures in that table are sitting next to a live reform proposal to remove them, silence on that point isn't scope discipline, it's an omission that would look, to a sophisticated reader (the exact audience this page targets — a US founder/CFO), like this page either doesn't know about the reform or is quietly hoping the reader doesn't find out from a sibling page. Neither is a good look for a firm claiming dual CA/US-CPA precision.
- **Why one sentence, not a full callout box.** Matching Project Office's lighter inline treatment (rather than Branch/Liaison's boxed treatment) keeps this page's synthesis-layer scope intact — it states the fact, states plainly it's not law, and links to the two sibling pages that carry the full regulatory-watch detail, rather than re-deriving that detail here. This is the same link-out discipline the rest of the page already uses for cost/timeline and FDI-channel depth.
- **Where it doesn't need adding:** the table itself. I considered adding a footnote/asterisk directly to the table's FDI/establishment-route row but judged that would clutter a table Stage 6 specifically wanted to stay a clean, scannable artifact — the pointer sentence sits immediately below the table's companion bullet list instead, where a reader working through the Branch/Liaison/Project Office decision will actually see it before acting on the numbers.
