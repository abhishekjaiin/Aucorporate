## Minimum Coverage List
(What every credible competing page covers — the page must address these or it will look incomplete against the field. Deliberately excludes the unresolved BO validity/renewal cycle per the guardrail above.)

1. Clear definition of a Branch Office and its basic purpose/eligibility framing
2. RBI/FEMA as the statutory and regulatory basis, naming FEMA 22(R)/2016 (currently operative — see Stage 3 orchestrator confirmation) as the governing regulation
3. Eligibility criteria: net-worth threshold and profit-track-record requirement under the current (2016) framework
4. The Automatic route vs Approval route distinction, and the AD Category-I Bank's role as the application/reporting channel
5. Form FNC named as the application form, with the process/documents checklist
6. A processing timeline
7. Permitted activities list
8. Prohibited activities list (retail trading, manufacturing outside SEZ, practicing Indian law)
9. MCA/ROC registration obligations — Companies Act 2013 §380, Form FC-1, and the 30-day filing window
10. Annual Activity Certificate (AAC) named as the ongoing compliance obligation
11. Closure/winding-up process overview
12. Cross-reference/linking to Liaison Office and Project Office as related but distinct structures

## Differentiation List
(What almost nobody in the analyzed set covers well, or covers at all — the real opportunity. Each item traces to a specific Stage 3 finding.)

1. **Regulation-numbered specificity beyond a name-drop** — cite FEMA 22(R)/2016 by actual regulation number (e.g., Regulation 4 for eligibility) the way only setindiabiz does among the 5; the other four either don't cite a number or weren't confirmed to.
2. **Route-determination as a decision rule, not just vocabulary** — explain which principal-business sectors put an applicant on the Automatic route vs which trigger the Approval route, the way only vjmglobal was confirmed to do.
3. **Regulator-side timeline instead of vendor-side turnaround** — state the AD bank's and RBI's actual review/decision windows (vjmglobal's confirmed pattern: AD bank ~5 working days, RBI ~4–6 weeks total), not the consultancy's internal document-processing time (setindiabiz's confirmed, reader-unhelpful pattern).
4. **AAC filing-routing specificity** — the AD bank + Directorate General of Income Tax (International Taxation) dual-routing detail, tied correctly to fiscal-year-end timing, confirmed only on companiesnext among the 5.
5. **Genuinely non-obvious procedural edge cases** — e.g., an Indian subsidiary of a foreign company cannot itself open a BO under the automatic route; a Project Office doesn't require a UIN the way a BO/LO does. Confirmed only in vjmglobal's supporting blog, not surfaced on its own main service page or on any of the other four.
6. **Tax treatment integrated into the main registration/decision page** — effective rate (~36.4–38.22% inclusive of surcharge/cess), PE characterization, MAT, transfer pricing, DTAA interaction, no withholding on head-office remittance — confirmed absent from all 5 registration-focused pages; siloed elsewhere in the competitive set (e.g., PwC's separate tax-reference tool per Stage 1).
7. **A real BO vs LO vs PO vs subsidiary decision framework** — none of the 5 combine "how to register a BO" with "why you'd choose a BO over the alternatives for your specific business model"; the cluster pattern (parallel BO/LO/PO pages) answers "how," not "whether."
8. **A properly developed restricted-country/sensitive-sector section** — Pakistan/China/Bangladesh/Sri Lanka/Afghanistan/Hong Kong/Macau nationality scrutiny, border-state (J&K, North-East, Andaman & Nicobar) location scrutiny, and Defence/Telecom/Private Security/Information & Broadcasting sector scrutiny — named as a recurring question everywhere per Stage 1, but Stage 3 found it "named-but-underdeveloped" on all 5 analyzed pages.
9. **Visible named author/reviewer credential on a commercial page** — only taxguru.in (non-commercial, contributed-article model) shows this; none of the 4 commercial/service-page competitors in the set do. A credentialed named author/reviewer on an actual service page would be a genuine first-mover trust signal in this specific set.
10. **Explicit dated currency statement + correct current-vs-draft framing** — only india-briefing.com is confirmed doing this (dated May 13, 2026; correctly states 2016 rules remain in force). Matching this framing while adding the operational depth (Form FNC, AD-bank timeline, AAC, closure) that Stage 3 found india-briefing itself may be thin on would combine two strengths no single analyzed page has together.
11. **Honest, explicit treatment of the unresolved BO validity/renewal question** — no analyzed page was confirmed to acknowledge that BO- vs LO-specific renewal rules appear conflated across public sources. Per Stage 3's directive to Stage 9, the accurate framing (pending fact-check) is that a BO continues "for as long as the underlying approval and AAC compliance remain in good standing," rather than asserting a specific numeric cycle. Stating this transparently — rather than silently picking one unverified version, as the field appears to do — is itself a trust differentiator, not just a compliance nicety.

## Unanswered PAA/Related Questions
(Pulled from Stage 1's PAA-style question set; cross-checked against what the 5 analyzed pages were actually confirmed to resolve.)

- **"How long is a branch office registration valid, and can it be renewed/extended?"** — Genuinely unresolved across the field, not just unaddressed by these 5; the one primary-source check run in this pipeline (Stage 3 orchestrator) found renewal figures that attach to Liaison Offices, not confirmed for Branch Offices. No analyzed page confirmed disambiguating this. (Must route through Stage 9 fact-check before this page states anything specific — see guardrail above.)
- **"Can a company from Pakistan, China or Bangladesh open a branch office in India?"** — Named repeatedly as a PAA-style question and as a recurring entity in Stage 1, but Stage 3 found no analyzed page developing it into a real, usable section.
- **"What is the difference between a branch office and a subsidiary company in India?"** — Exists on the field only as separate, standalone comparison pages (e.g., india-briefing's distinct "Rep Office / BO / PO vs LLP/Subsidiary" page), not integrated with the "how to register a BO" content itself. A reader in commercial-investigation mode researching BO registration is not confirmed to get this comparison in the same page.
- **"Who approves a branch office in India — RBI or MCA?"** — Partially answered across the set, but Stage 1 flags this as a real confusion point (RBI approves the establishment; MCA/ROC separately registers it as a foreign company under Companies Act §380). No analyzed page was confirmed to disambiguate the two-authority structure crisply in one place.
- Note: Stage 2 explicitly found **no evidence of cost/fee-specific search demand** ("branch office registration India cost/fees") — this is flagged there as a gap with no confirmed evidence, not asserted here as a real unanswered question; do not manufacture a pricing section on the strength of this note alone.
