2. **Meta description:** "RBI licensing for fintech companies entering India" → "RBI licensing to set up a fintech company in India" — same character count (163), now contains the literal primary keyword.
3. **Metadata note (inline documentation):** corrected the trim-length claim from "~156 characters" to the actual 163, and documented both this stage's meta edit and the character-count correction, so the record stays accurate for anyone reviewing later stages.
4. **Five RBI Portals table (FIRMS row):** anchor text "US company hub" → "FEMA compliance page" — the link target is a sub-page, not the hub; the old anchor text misdescribed the destination.
5. **Post-License section:** added one parenthetical sentence linking to `/india-business-setup/regulatory-compliance` ("our regulatory compliance guide") — this was in Stage 6's approved outbound-linking plan but was missing from the draft entirely.
6. **FAQ section:** converted all 9 bold question lines to `###` (H3) headings — strengthens heading hierarchy and FAQ-rich-result formatting; no wording changed in either questions or answers.

No factual claims, hedges, figures, or Writer's Notes content were touched.

## Snippet/FAQ Opportunities Identified

- The FAQ's first two questions ("Can a foreign company get an RBI NBFC license in India?" and "What license does a foreign fintech need to operate in India?") are the strongest featured-snippet candidates on the page — they match the two question-keywords Stage 2 flagged as genuinely unanswered elsewhere in the competitive set. Their opening 1–2 sentences already form a reasonably tight, self-contained direct answer (roughly 50–65 words) before the elaboration continues — close enough to the 40–60 word snippet sweet spot that no trim was made; shortening further would cut the category-specific precision (NOF-by-category, license-by-business-model) that's this page's actual differentiator.
- Converting the FAQ questions to real `###` headings (see Edits Made #6) materially improves FAQPage schema/rich-result eligibility versus the bold-text version — this was the single highest-value fix in this pass.
- No dedicated 40-60 word snippet block exists near the very top of the page (the intro paragraph is intentionally a longer rhetorical hook, not a direct-answer paragraph). That's fine — the FAQ carries the snippet opportunity for this page, consistent with how the target queries ("can a foreign company get an RBI NBFC license," "what license does a foreign fintech need") are phrased as questions, not as the page's title-level query.
