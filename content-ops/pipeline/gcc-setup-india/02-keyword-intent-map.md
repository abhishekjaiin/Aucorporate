## Foreign/International Framing Check
Evidence found for international-audience phrasing:
1. Stage 1 SERP research (Freshness Signals section) recorded a direct competitor title: "GCC Setup in India — Complete Guide for US & UK Companies (2026)" (KRP Associates).
2. This stage's supplementary web search found four more direct/near-direct competitors using explicit "for Foreign Companies" framing in titles/H1s: stratrich.com ("Setting Up a GCC in India for Foreign Companies Guide"), masllp.com ("Setting Up a GCC in India: Full Guide for Foreign Companies"), and manishanilgupta.com (two separate assets: "GCC Setup in India: Complete Guide for Foreign Companies (2026)" and "GCC Setup in India: Complete FAQ Guide for Foreign Companies (2026)").

Conclusion: this is real title-level SERP evidence, not an assumption about AU Corp's audience — but it is a secondary/qualifier pattern layered onto the broad "GCC setup in India" head term, never the sole framing of a page that outranks the unqualified competitors. Recommendation: do not make "for foreign companies" or "international" part of the primary keyword/H1. Do use "GCC setup in India for foreign companies" as a Secondary keyword — an H2 subsection or FAQ entry (e.g., "Can a foreign company set up a GCC in India?") — since at least 5 competitors now demonstrably target that exact phrase-level variant. GIFT City/IFSC content, where it touches cross-border structuring, may also naturally carry this framing without needing to force it into the primary term.
