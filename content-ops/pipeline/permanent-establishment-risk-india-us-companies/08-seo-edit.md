## Edits Made

1. **Meta description shortened** (Metadata block, top of file): from ~187 characters to ~153 characters. Removed "thresholds" and "or subsidiary" (subsidiary is covered by "EOR" as the harder of the two questions and is still addressed in-page; the description was over length and needed a genuine trim, not a keyword change). Primary keyword, all four differentiators, and click-worthy framing preserved.
   - Before: "How US companies trigger permanent establishment risk in India — the four PE types, DTAA Article 5 thresholds, key case law, and whether an EOR or subsidiary actually eliminates exposure."
   - After: "How US companies trigger permanent establishment risk in India — the four PE types, DTAA Article 5, key case law, and whether an EOR eliminates exposure."

2. **Intro, sentence 1**: "Permanent establishment (PE) exposure is a function of..." → "Permanent establishment (PE) risk is a function of..." Brings the primary-keyword concept (permanent establishment + risk) into the first ~30 words rather than the ~90-word mark, without changing meaning or adding a word count. "Exposure" is used extensively elsewhere in the page, so this doesn't create repetition — it's a straight swap of one instance already using a synonym.

3. **Typology section intro**: "each responds to a different kind of mitigation" → "each calls for a different way to avoid or mitigate it." Folds in the "avoid PE risk India" secondary keyword (real SERP evidence per Stage 2, thin in the original draft) as a natural synonym pairing rather than a bolted-on phrase — reads the same, costs nothing stylistically.

No other changes made. The draft was already close to the checklist bar; over-editing a page this clean would be the failure mode, not the fix.
