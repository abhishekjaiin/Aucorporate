## The Startup India Tax-Incentive Call (Stage 4's open decision)

**Decision: exclude from this page.** Reasoning:

1. **Scope boundary.** STATUS.md defines this page as "compare registration/entity options, understand the real process" — a registration-process page, not a tax-incentive page. Section 80-IAC/56 is a post-incorporation tax-planning question, one step removed from what this reader is asking.
2. **Existing-page ownership.** The hub page (`india-entry-for-us-companies`) and `transfer-pricing-us-india-subsidiary` already own the cluster's India-side tax material (concessional rate election, Section 482/161-173, Form 3CEB, APAs). A Startup India tax-incentive mention would sit awkwardly between two owners rather than cleanly inside either.
3. **Weak keyword evidence.** It's not present anywhere in Stage 2's primary, secondary, or long-tail keyword sets — it surfaced only in Stage 3's competitor tally (1 of 6 pages, vjmglobal) and Stage 4 itself flagged it as "caution, not recommended without Stage 5 sign-off."
4. **Real risk of overclaiming.** DPIIT Startup India recognition and the Section 80-IAC exemption have genuine eligibility friction for a foreign-parent-owned wholly-owned subsidiary (the "genuine innovation/scalability" test and the incorporation-age/turnover caps are not automatic for a US subsidiary that's really a market-entry vehicle rather than an independent Indian startup). A light-touch mention risks implying broader eligibility than typically holds, which is the exact "advertising rather than expertise" failure mode this brief is designed to avoid.
5. If AU Corporate wants to own this angle credibly, it deserves its own scoped treatment (eligibility criteria stated honestly, including where a foreign subsidiary is likely to be excluded) — that's a future, separate piece of content, not a bolt-on here.

## Explicit Guidance for the Writer

- **Tone: practical expertise, written like someone who has actually filed these forms** — not "AU Corporate offers comprehensive India entry solutions." The existing sibling pages are a good tone reference (e.g., "the real drivers are..."; "it's almost never the Indian-side filing that slows things down"); match that register, not brochure copy.
- **Do not re-derive a cost or timeline figure.** Use the existing cluster's numbers verbatim if citing any (4-6 weeks Automatic Route incorporation, 8-12 weeks banked/funded/operational, ₹55,000-1,15,000+ combined setup cost) and link to `cost-timeline-incorporate-company-india-from-us` for the breakdown, rather than writing a second independent estimate that could quietly drift from the first.
- **Do not rebuild the nominee-director mechanics or the apostille process from scratch.** State the rule/requirement in a sentence or two, frame it as a real gating question, then link to `how-to-incorporate-subsidiary-india-from-us` for the worked answer.
- **The 5-way entity table is the one place to genuinely go deep** — this is the section that doesn't exist anywhere else on the site yet. Selection criteria should map to business activity (revenue-generating operations vs. representative-only vs. time-bound project vs. lighter compliance appetite), not just restate legal definitions.
- **Give the FC-1 path (Branch/Liaison/Project Office) real, equal-looking space in the decision table** — not a footnote after a subsidiary-focused wall of text. This is a specific, evidenced gap (Stage 4 confirms it's a bolt-on everywhere else in the field, and it's still true across AU's own site outside the single deep subsidiary-vs-branch page).
- **Cite primary sources by name** where the page makes a factual claim: mca.gov.in / SPICe+, Companies Act 2013 s.149(3) and s.164, RBI FIRMS portal, FEMA (Non-Debt Instruments) Rules — this is a genuinely absent pattern across the competitor set per Stage 4 and costs nothing to do consistently.
- **State the SPICe+ Part A / legacy RUN-INC-1 correction once, plainly**, as a factual clarification, not a "look how current we are" freshness flex.
- **No service-line shopping list.** If a paragraph exists only to mention a service AU offers rather than to answer the reader's actual question at that point in the page, cut it. The one legitimate place for AU's services to appear as such is a short, honest closing section/CTA (consultation-led, matching the category's non-transactional intent per Stage 2) — not woven into the process/comparison content itself.
- **Watch for promotional drift specifically in**: the entity-comparison section (risk: turning selection criteria into "and that's why you should use AU Corporate" rather than genuinely helping the reader decide), and any post-incorporation mention (risk: turning "here's what comes next" into an Accounting/Payroll/Virtual CFO pitch). Keep both descriptive and reader-first.
- **Internal linking is not optional decoration here — it's load-bearing.** Given how much of Stage 4's differentiation list is already answered elsewhere in the cluster, this page's credibility (and its ability to avoid becoming a shallow, thin-content page) depends on confidently routing readers to the five sibling pages named above at the right moments, not on restating their content.
