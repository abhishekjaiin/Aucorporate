## AU Corporate existing-coverage check (confirms/corrects the pipeline premise)
Read directly from the repo:
- `/app/india-entry-for-us-companies/page.tsx` — this hub page covers entity choice (subsidiary vs. branch), incorporation mechanics/apostille, **ongoing FEMA/RBI filings** (FC-GPR, FC-TRS, annual FLA), and transfer pricing (Section 482, Form 3CEB/Form 48, APAs). It links to a dedicated `/india-entry-for-us-companies/fema-compliance-us-company-india-subsidiary` sub-page. It does **not** mention AOC-4, MGT-7, DIR-3 KYC, ADT-1, board meeting cadence, AGM timing, or a consolidated annual filing calendar anywhere.
- `/app/india-business-setup/regulatory-compliance/page.tsx` — a general, non-US-specific "six regimes" overview page. Its "Corporate Governance" card mentions AOC-4 and MGT-7/7A **in one sentence each**, plus board-meeting cadence and AGM requirement, but has no DIR-3 KYC, no ADT-1, no deadline dates, and no calendar/checklist structure. It's framed around domestic Companies Act compliance generally, not the foreign-subsidiary-specific annual rhythm (and isn't part of the US-company cluster).

**Premise confirmed, with a precise correction for later stages**: AU has no page anywhere on the site that assembles the ROC annual filing calendar (AOC-4, MGT-7, ADT-1, DIR-3 KYC, board meetings, AGM) into one place, and nothing that frames it for a US parent's fiscal/DIN-holder concerns specifically. What AU *does* already own well is the FEMA/RBI annual-filing angle (FLA return, FC-GPR/FC-TRS) via the existing FEMA sub-page — the new page should cross-link to that rather than re-explain it, and should differentiate itself as the *Companies Act / MCA-ROC* side of the annual calendar, not duplicate the FEMA side.

## Recurring Entities & Regulations
Named/cited consistently across ranking content:
- **Companies Act, 2013** (the base framework) — Sections 92, 96, 129, 134, 137, 139(6), 141, 403, 392 all cited by name in different articles
- **MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs)** and **ROC (Registrar of Companies)** — the administering bodies for all corporate filings
- **Form AOC-4** (financial statements, due 30 days from AGM) and **Form MGT-7 / MGT-7A** (annual return, due 60 days from AGM; MGT-7A for "small companies" under Sec. 2(85))
- **Form DIR-3 KYC** (director KYC) — recurrence high, but **frequency is disputed across ranking content**: most sources describe it as an annual filing due September 30; at least one ranking Khanna & Associates title explicitly claims "DIR-3 KYC Now Every 3 Years – New Rules" — this is a direct conflict observed in the SERP itself, not just uncertainty on my part. **Flagged as unverified and needs primary-source (MCA) confirmation before Stage 6 commits to a specific cadence claim.**
- **Form ADT-1** (auditor appointment) — due 15 days from the appointing meeting; first auditor appointment within 30 days of incorporation; one source notes ADT-1 for first-auditor appointments became mandatory "on or after July 14, 2025" — a recent procedural change worth verifying
- **Form MBP-1** (director's disclosure of interest, filed at first board meeting) and **Form FC-1** (foreign company registration filing, within 30 days of incorporation) — appear in Companies Act compliance checklists
- **Form DPT-3** and **MSME-1** — mentioned as part of the fuller annual filing set in at least one penalty-focused article
- **FEMA, 1999** and the **RBI** — Form FC-GPR (share allotment, 30-day window), Form FC-TRS (share transfer), and the **Annual FLA Return** (Foreign Liabilities and Assets, filed via the RBI's FLAIR system) — deadline cited as **July 15** across multiple sources (note: AU's own existing FEMA sub-page and this SERP research agree on FLA being the most commonly missed recurring filing)
- **Companies Compliance Facilitation Scheme (CCFS), 2026** — a live, current MCA scheme (effective April 15–July 15, 2026, extended to August 31, 2026 per General Circular 03/2026) offering a 90% waiver on additional filing fees for companies with pending ROC filings; explicitly stated to apply to foreign-owned/foreign-subsidiary companies (excluding foreign companies with pending FC-3/FC-4). This is a timely, current-events angle several competitors are actively covering (Khanna & Associates, TaxGuru, Setindiabiz, PRSR & Co, CorpLawUpdates, GiftCFO all have dedicated CCFS-2026 articles) that AU has no coverage of at all.
- **Transfer pricing** — Sections 92–92F of the Income-tax Act cited for arm's-length pricing on intercompany transactions (consistent with AU's own existing page, which already covers Section 482/Form 3CEB from the US side)
- **Income tax** — corporate tax rate for foreign companies cited at 40% + surcharge/cess; income tax return due October 31
- **GST** — GSTR-1 monthly/quarterly filings cited as part of the fuller compliance load where applicable
- **TDS** — monthly deduction/deposit obligations, due 7th of the following month
- **DPDP Act, 2023** — appears in broader "compliance checklist" content as a newer obligation layer (consistent with AU's own regulatory-compliance page)
- **Labour Codes** (four consolidated codes) — not prominent in the specific annual-compliance-calendar SERP results, but present in AU's own existing regulatory-compliance page; worth a cross-check by Stage 4 on whether competitors are folding labour-code changes into their 2026 compliance-calendar content

## Search Intent (page-level read)
Mixed **informational + commercial** intent, skewing toward commercial-informational (top-of-funnel guide content published by firms that sell the filings/advisory being described), based on:
- A large share of ranking domains are compliance-filing SaaS platforms or filing agencies (ClearTax, RegisterKaro, IncorpX, eBizFiling, IndiaFilings, LegalWiz, TMWala, Corpbiz, Kanakkupillai, Setindiabiz, FilingPoint) whose "guide" content sits directly next to a priced service (IndiaFilings explicitly prices "Company Annual Filing – ROC Compliance Starts at ₹1,999" on a page ranking for this cluster) — evidence of transactional intent capture dressed as informational content.
- A comparable share are CA firms, CS practitioners, and law firms (Ahlawat & Associates, Manish Anil Gupta, Khanna & Associates, KMG & Co, Jordensky, Treelife, PKC, DSRV, Jain Anurag & Associates, Chhota CFO, GiftCFO) publishing the same category of guide/checklist content as advisory lead-gen, not as a standalone media product.
- A smaller slice are pure informational publishers/aggregators (Mondaq, Lexology, TaxGuru, Wikipedia) republishing or summarizing law-firm content with no direct sale attached.
- No purely navigational or purely transactional results (e.g., a pricing/signup page ranking with no educational framing) were observed — every ranking page leads with education/checklist/calendar framing even where a service sits behind it. This suggests the winning content format for this query cluster is a comprehensive, dated guide/calendar — not a thin service page — which matches AU's existing site pattern on the `/india-entry-for-us-companies/` cluster.

## Freshness Signals
This is a fast-moving, currency-sensitive topic; ranking content skews recent and several pages update annually:
- Multiple titles explicitly carry "2026" or "FY 2025–26" / "2025-26" tags (RegisterKaro "Foreign Subsidiary of Indian Company Compliance 2026," eBizFiling "ROC Annual Compliance Calendar...FY 2025–26," Khanna & Associates "...Guide 2026," Jordensky "...2025 Guide")
- Active regulatory-change coverage observed: the MCA extended the AOC-4/MGT-7 due date for FY 2024-25 to 31 January 2026 (after an earlier extension to 31 December 2025) — multiple competitors have dedicated "due date extension" news posts, meaning this SERP rewards near-real-time updates, not just an annual refresh.
- The Companies Compliance Facilitation Scheme, 2026 (April–August 2026 window) is a live current-events hook that several competitors are actively capturing and AU has zero coverage of.
- ADT-1 mandatory-filing change for first auditor appointments "on or after July 14, 2025" is cited as a recent procedural shift.
- DIR-3 KYC cadence dispute (annual vs. every-3-years, see Recurring Entities section) is itself a freshness/accuracy risk — if the cadence genuinely changed recently, older "annual" content across many competitor sites may already be stale, which is either a risk or an opportunity for AU depending on which claim is correct once verified.
- No AY/FY-specific stale content was directly observed (e.g., nothing found still citing FY 2022-23 as current), but this is a limited-sample observation given WebFetch was unavailable for direct date-stamp verification.
