## Regulatory Currency Check

- **Income-tax Act, 1961 vs. 2025**: The draft's PE/business-connection citation is stale — it cites the 1961 Act's Section 9(1)(i) when AU Corporate's own live site has already transitioned to citing the Income-tax Act, 2025 (effective 1 April 2026, which has now passed as of today's date). This is a real omission-by-staleness issue, not just an unverified figure — flagged above as a MUST FIX.
- **RBI's draft 2025 Establishment Regulations**: Correctly treated by the draft as not yet notified/not yet law, with FEMA 22(R)/2016-RB as the operative framework. This matches all available reporting through the most recent sources found in this session. No superseded-process issue here — the draft's framing is current and appropriately hedged.
- **Government-route country list**: The draft's framing of the Bangladesh/Sri Lanka/Afghanistan/Iran/China/Hong Kong/Macau rule as unconditional (rather than conditional on J&K/NE/A&N location) is not a "superseded rule" issue but a present-tense mischaracterization of a rule that appears to still be in force in its (conditional) form — flagged above as a MUST FIX.
- No other outdated process (e.g., a since-abolished filing step, a renamed authority, a since-changed form number) was identified in the draft. The AAC dual-routing, Form FC-1/FC-2, Form 15CA/15CB, and closure sequence all appear to reflect the current process as best this session's sourcing could confirm.
