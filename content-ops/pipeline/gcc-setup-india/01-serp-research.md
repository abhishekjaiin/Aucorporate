## Recurring Entities & Regulations
Named explicitly across ranking pages/snippets:
- **FEMA** (Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999) — governs foreign investment into a GCC entity
- **RBI** — FDI reporting via banking channel, **FIRC** (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate), **Annual Return on Foreign Liabilities and Assets (FLA)**
- **FDI automatic route** (up to 100% in most GCC-relevant sectors) vs. government-approval route for restricted sectors
- **Companies Act** (implied via "Private Limited Company" as the standard incorporation vehicle) — not seen with explicit section citations
- **SEZ Act** / **SEZ status** (customs duty exemptions, cited in the GIFT City cluster)
- **IFSCA** (International Financial Services Centres Authority) — unified regulator for IFSC entities, cited as replacing fragmented RBI/SEBI/IRDAI oversight for GIFT City-based GCCs
- **GST** — cited in GIFT City tax-benefit content
- **MAT** (Minimum Alternate Tax) — reduced-rate benefit cited for IFSC entities
- **Union Budget 2026-27** — tax holiday to 2047 for foreign cloud providers; unified 15.5% safe-harbour margin for IT services (cited in a February/March 2026 news search)
- **NASSCOM** and **Zinnov** recur repeatedly as the primary data/authority sources cited by nearly every competitor (GCC counts, revenue, employment figures)
- Notably **not observed** in any ranking page/snippet: DPIIT, Invest India, MCA (by name), GST portal, DTAA — these did not surface as named entities in this research pass, which may indicate a content gap or simply that they weren't captured by the synthesized search results (flagged as open question below).

## Search Intent (page-level read)
- **Informational-led, with commercial undertone**: the overwhelming majority of ranking pages are long-form "how to / what is / cost of / best cities for" guides published by firms that sell GCC-adjacent services (advisory, EOR, managed-office, staffing, BOT). This is classic top-of-funnel content marketing — informational in format, commercial in publisher intent — evidenced by every non-Big-4, non-NASSCOM domain being a vendor blog.
- **Explicitly commercial/transactional**: EY, PwC (and by extension likely KPMG, Deloitte, ANSR, Table Space, GoodWorks, managed-GCC providers) run dedicated service pages alongside their blogs — clear "talk to us" positioning.
- **Purely informational, non-commercial**: NASSCOM community guides, Wikipedia's "Global capability center" article, Mondaq's syndicated legal-framework piece, and news/PR items (Yahoo Finance, GlobeNewswire, PIB) — no service being sold.
- **No navigational intent observed** — no brand-name queries returned in this research pass (expected, since this is a category topic, not a branded one).
