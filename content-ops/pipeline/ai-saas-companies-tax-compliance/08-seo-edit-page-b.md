## Edits Made

1. **Intro paragraph — added primary keyword phrase naturally.** Changed "This guide walks through how AI, SaaS and technology companies actually make that call..." to "This guide walks through India entry for AI, SaaS and technology companies: how that call actually gets made..." Reason: the primary keyword ("India entry for SaaS companies") did not appear anywhere in the first ~100 words in any form — the paragraph used "set up in India" instead. This edit places a natural variant of the phrase inside a sentence that was already previewing the page's scope, without disrupting flow or adding a new clause. No other wording in the paragraph was touched.

No other edits were made. The draft already reads naturally, follows the approved architecture's structure and internal-linking plan precisely, and does not show signs of over-optimization — per the guardrail against editing for its own sake, no further changes were made.
