## GIFT City / IFSC Placement Decision
**Recommendation: brief mention with a pointer to a future dedicated page — not a full comparison row, not excluded entirely.**

Reasoning: GIFT City recurred independently across Stage 1's related-search list and appears as a standalone strong sub-cluster (stratrich.com and treelife.in/gift.treelife.in both rank specifically for GIFT City/IFSC content, separate from their general India-entry content). But it is a specific SEZ under a distinct regulator (IFSCA, combining RBI/SEBI/IRDAI/PFRDA powers) with its own incentive regime (stamp duty exemption, 10-year tax holiday, 9% MAT) — not a general-purpose state registration option. Stage 1 explicitly warned that treating it as one row alongside Maharashtra/Karnataka/Gujarat "would misrepresent it." Given this piece is blog-tier and scoped to state-level comparison, the right move is:
- One short callout box or paragraph acknowledging GIFT City as the answer to "what about a specialized zone instead of a state" (this also naturally answers the "GIFT City IFSC company registration benefits foreign company" related search)
- Explicit framing: "GIFT City isn't a state option, it's a specialized financial services zone — see [future dedicated page] for details"
- No comparison-table row, no stamp-duty/tax-holiday figures treated with the same rigor this piece applies to actual states (that level of detail belongs to its own page, per Stage 1's flag)
- Flag to Stage 6/implementation: a dedicated GIFT City/IFSC page is a real content gap (confirmed by Stage 1: AU's three existing india-business-setup pages have zero GIFT City mentions) and should be queued as a future standalone topic

## Foreign/International Framing Check
Evidence found for international-audience phrasing — real, but thinner than the generic query, and should stay secondary:
- Two direct competitors title pages explicitly around "foreign": beaconfiling.com ("Best Indian States for Foreign Business") and jordensky.com ("Best States in India to Set Up Your Foreign Company")
- ebizfiling.com ranks for "Best Indian Cities for Foreigners to Register a Company" — but this is a city-level query, not state-level, so it doesn't directly support "foreign" framing on the state-comparison primary keyword
- Stage 1 logged "best state for foreign company setting up in India" and "state incentives India foreign company industrial policy subsidies" as related-search queries actually run that returned meaningfully relevant results
- A recurring (paraphrased, not verbatim-confirmed) PAA-adjacent framing: "What is the best state for foreign companies to set up in India?"

Against this, the **majority** of the top-ranking pages (Urban Company, UKIBC, Wikipedia, MCA portal, Outlook Business, IndiaDataMap — 6 of 15 classified sources) use fully generic, nationality-agnostic framing with no "foreign" qualifier at all, and the SERP as a whole reads as dominated by the plain "which state" query, not a "which state for foreign companies" query.

**Verdict:** keep the H1/primary keyword and title generic ("best state to register a company in India"). The foreign-investor angle is real and evidence-backed enough to earn a dedicated H2/FAQ subsection targeting "best state for foreign company to register in India" directly (since AU Corporate's actual audience is foreign investors, this subsection is where audience and query legitimately meet) — but it must not be inserted into the H1, meta title, or primary heading structure, where the generic query has the stronger and broader evidence base. This is the one place in this map where "foreign" framing is justified by evidence rather than assumed from audience.
