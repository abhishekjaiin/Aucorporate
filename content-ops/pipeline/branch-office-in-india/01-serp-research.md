## Recurring Entities & Regulations
Named explicitly and repeatedly across ranking pages:
- **FEMA, 1999** — Section 6(6), the statutory basis for BO/LO/PO regulation
- **FEMA 22(R)/2016** ("Foreign Exchange Management (Establishment in India of a Branch Office or a Liaison Office or a Project Office or any other place of business) Regulations, 2016") — the currently-governing regulation named on multiple pages, including income tax dept and law-firm sources
- **RBI Master Direction / Master Circular** on Establishment of BO/LO/PO (e.g., Master Circular No. 7/2012-13; Master Direction referenced by several CA-firm blogs)
- **Draft FEMA (Establishment in India of a Branch or Office) Regulations, 2025** — released 3 Oct 2025, proposed replacement of the 2016 framework; introduces "Entity Resident Outside India (EROI)" concept, consolidates BO/LO into "Branch"/"Office," removes minimum net-worth/profit-track-record eligibility criteria, streamlines UIN allotment, simplifies closure. Public comments were sought via RBI's "Connect 2 Regulate" portal by 24 Oct 2025. **Status as of research date is not confirmed finalized/notified** — this is a critical freshness item for later agents to verify before publishing regulatory claims (see Open Questions).
- **AD Category-I Bank** — the authorised dealer bank through which all applications and reporting are routed
- **Form FNC** — the application form filed with the AD bank for BO/LO/PO establishment
- **Automatic route vs. Approval route** — RBI's two-track approval mechanism based on FDI sector eligibility
- **Government-consultation/approval cases** — applicants from Pakistan, or from Bangladesh/Sri Lanka/Afghanistan/Iran/China/Hong Kong/Macau proposing an office in J&K, North-East, or Andaman & Nicobar; or in Defence, Telecom, Private Security, or Information & Broadcasting sectors; also land-border-country restrictions (China, Pakistan, Nepal, Bangladesh, etc. per Press Note 3/2020-style scrutiny referenced in some snippets)
- **Companies Act, 2013 — Section 380**, and **Form FC-1** (initial registration with ROC within 30 days) / **Form FC-2** (subsequent changes/extension intimation)
- **ROC / MCA** registration obligations for the BO as a "foreign company" establishment under the Companies Act
- **UIN (Unique Identification Number)** allotted to the BO
- **Annual Activity Certificate (AAC)** — CA-certified annual filing confirming activities stayed within RBI-approved scope, due with audited accounts by 30 September (or within 6 months of a non-March year-end), copied to the Directorate General of Income Tax (International Taxation)
- **Validity period** — commonly cited as an initial approval window (search snippets show inconsistent framing: some sources say ~3 years before extension is required, others imply renewal is "generally not required" for BOs specifically vs. LOs — this inconsistency should be resolved/verified before Stage 2/3 treat it as settled fact)
- **Permitted activities** (as named on ranking pages): export/import of goods, acting as buying/selling agent, IT services/software development, professional/consultancy services, research work in the parent's area of business, promoting technical/financial collaborations, providing technical support for parent-company products, representing foreign airline/shipping lines
- **Prohibited activities**: retail trading; manufacturing or processing (except within an SEZ); practicing Indian law
- **Tax treatment**: BO taxed as a "foreign company" — corporate rate cited as reduced from 40% to 35% effective April 2025 (Union Budget 2025), plus surcharge (2% >₹1 crore income, 5% >₹10 crore) and 4% health & education cess, giving an effective range cited as ~36.4%–38.22%; BO treated as a Permanent Establishment (PE); Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT) cited around 15.6–16.38%; no withholding tax on remittance of branch profits to head office; transfer pricing provisions apply to international transactions with the parent/associated enterprises
- **DTAA** referenced generically in foreign-company-taxation content, though not deeply detailed in the snippets reviewed
- **Closure process**: intimation via AD Category-I bank to RBI (Regional Office for LOs, Central Office for BOs), auditor's certificate, tax clearance, no pending litigation/liabilities confirmation, bank account closure, ROC de-registration (Form FC-2 referenced in one source for this step)

## Freshness Signals
- **Regulatory framework is mid-transition.** The currently-cited governing regulation is FEMA 22(R)/2016, but RBI released **draft** replacement regulations (Establishment in India of a Branch or Office Regulations, 2025) on 3 October 2025, with public comment sought through 24 October 2025. Coverage of this draft (EY alert, India Briefing, Lexology, Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan, Stratrich) is recent (dated late 2025). A search for a final/notified 2026 version did **not** turn up a confirmed notification specific to branch/liaison/project office establishment (a different, unrelated FEMA regulation — Authorised Persons Regulations, 2026 — was notified in April/May 2026, but that is not the same regulation). **This must be verified before publishing** — see Open Questions.
- **Tax rate is a recent, verifiable change**: foreign-company/branch corporate tax rate reduction from 40% to 35% is dated to the April 2025 (Union Budget 2025) change and is consistently cited across multiple independent sources (PwC, indiacompanyincorporation.com, npahilwani.com, uja.in), giving reasonable confidence this is current.
- India-briefing.com's URL slug explicitly includes "2026" and references the 2025 draft reforms, indicating that page has been actively updated for currency — a competitive freshness bar the new AU Corp page should meet or beat.
- Several ranking pages (lexology.com entry dated November 2024, various undated CA-firm blogs) show no clear "last updated" stamp in the snippets reviewed — genuine staleness could not be confirmed without direct page access (WebFetch was blocked), so this should be treated as unverified rather than assumed.
