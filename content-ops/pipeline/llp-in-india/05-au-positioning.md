# AU Corporate Positioning: LLP in India

## Relevant Service Lines for This Page
Only the service lines that a reader actually researching LLP incorporation/eligibility/compliance would need — grounded in the Minimum Coverage and Differentiation lists from `04-content-gap.md`, and checked against what AU Corporate's site already says elsewhere.

- **Company Incorporation / Business Setup** — this is the page's core topic (MCA incorporation via FiLLiP, RUN-LLP, DPIN/DSC, LLP Agreement/Form 3). It belongs here the same way it anchors `/india-business-setup/company-formation` — as the subject matter itself, not an add-on pitch. This page is the LLP-specific sibling of that pillar, not a different service.
- **FDI Advisory** — directly serves the differentiator Stage 4 flagged as the biggest real gap: the two-part FDI-in-LLP automatic-route gate (sector permits 100% automatic-route FDI for a company AND no FDI-linked performance conditions) and the practical decision sequence around it (does the sector qualify → do performance conditions apply → if not, what's the fallback). No competitor page walks this through practically; AU Corporate's FDI-channels practice (`/india-business-setup/fdi-channels`) already does this reasoning for company FDI and can extend the same logic to LLP capital contribution.
- **FEMA & RBI Compliance** — the FDI-in-LLP reporting obligation (Form FDI-LLP(I), 30-day window for capital contribution/profit-share transfer reporting) is a distinct FEMA filing from the company-route Form FC-GPR the site already documents elsewhere. This belongs here specifically because it's a different form/mechanic than what `/india-business-setup/company-formation` and `/services/taxation-regulatory` describe for companies — a reader who assumes "same FEMA filing as a Pvt Ltd" needs correcting, and this is exactly the kind of compliance-mechanics precision that differentiates AU Corporate's version from generic listicles.
- **Corporate Tax / International Tax** — the LLP tax-treatment differentiator: flat 30% rate, single-level/partnership taxation under Section 10(2A) of the Income-tax Act (partner's share of profit exempt from further tax), and the 18.5% Alternate Minimum Tax where deductions are claimed. This is a genuine right-to-win angle because AU Corporate's own `/services/taxation-regulatory` page already establishes the company-side comparison point (22% concessional corporate rate for most newly incorporated subsidiaries) — this page can extend that existing framing by explaining *why* the tax math is a real factor in the LLP-vs-Pvt-Ltd decision, not just stating both rates in isolation the way competitors do.
- **Compliance & Regulatory Services** — Form 8 (Statement of Account & Solvency) and Form 11 (Annual Return) annual filings, the audit threshold trigger (₹40 lakh turnover / ₹25 lakh partner contribution — flag to writer as market-consensus, not primary-source-verified per Stage 1/4), and DPIN/DSC prerequisites. This is table-stakes content the page must cover competently, and it's squarely inside AU Corporate's actual regulatory-compliance practice as shown on `/india-business-setup/regulatory-compliance` and `/services/taxation-regulatory`.
- **Audit & Assurance** — relevant narrowly, as the natural home for explaining the audit threshold trigger and what the Form 8 solvency statement actually requires — not as a pitch for AU Corporate's statutory audit service, just as the honest answer to "does my LLP need an audit."

## Service Lines Deliberately Excluded
- **GST Advisory & Compliance** — GST registration for an LLP is triggered by the same turnover thresholds as any Indian business entity; it is not LLP-specific and Stage 4's Minimum Coverage / Differentiation lists don't flag it as a topic this page needs to own. Forcing in a GST section here would duplicate `/services/taxation-regulatory` and `/india-business-setup/banking-taxation` without adding anything an LLP-specific reader needs. At most, one sentence noting GST works the same way as for any entity, with a link out — no dedicated section.
- **Transfer Pricing** — while an LLP with foreign partners can technically have international related-party transactions, no competitor page in Stage 1/3's analysis surfaces this as a distinct LLP concern, and Stage 4 doesn't flag it as a gap. Inserting a transfer-pricing section here would be forcing a service in because AU Corporate offers it, not because this reader needs it at the LLP-overview stage. Deep transfer-pricing content already lives on `/services/taxation-regulatory` and should stay there.
- **Business Valuation / Transaction Advisory** — the only plausible tie-in is LLP-to-Pvt-Ltd conversion, but conversion is a compliance/procedural question (Section 366, Form URC-1, SPICe+, INC-23/24) at this page's depth, not an M&A or valuation event. Pulling in Transaction Advisory here would misrepresent what the reader is actually asking.
- **Risk Advisory / Internal Controls** — no evidence anywhere in Stage 1-4 that this is a reader need for an LLP-overview page. These are enterprise-scale concerns unrelated to a founder or CFO deciding on/registering/maintaining an LLP.
- **Payroll** — not a differentiator or gap for this topic; employer registrations aren't LLP-specific and belong on the labour-law/regulatory-compliance content, not here.
- **Virtual CFO** — tempting as a generic "ongoing advisory relationship" upsell, but nothing in the gap analysis calls for it, and the page already has a natural retained-relationship narrative through annual compliance (Form 8/11) and tax filing — no need to name a separate service line for it.

## AU Corporate's Right-to-Win Angle
1. **Correcting the market-wide 120-day residency error, with the statutory source.** Stage 1/4 found four of five analyzed competitor pages (spanning a tax-filing SaaS, a registration platform, a law firm, and a fintech platform) still state the pre-2021 182-day resident-partner test. AU Corporate can state the current 120-day rule correctly and name the LLP (Amendment) Act, 2021 as the source — an accuracy edge that's checkable and specific, not a vague "we're more careful" claim.
2. **Connecting the FDI-in-LLP gate to an actual decision sequence**, not a static rule statement. AU Corporate's FDI-channels practice already reasons through automatic-route vs. government-route logic for company FDI (`/india-business-setup/fdi-channels`); extending that same practical sequencing to LLP capital contribution — is the sector open to 100% automatic-route FDI, do performance conditions apply, what's the fallback — is a natural extension of expertise the firm already demonstrates elsewhere on the site, not a claim invented for this page.
3. **The tax-math explanation behind the LLP-vs-Pvt-Ltd choice**, not just two numbers side by side. Because AU Corporate's own taxation-regulatory content already establishes the company-side concessional rate, this page can do what no competitor does: explain concretely why a flat 30% LLP rate with no dividend-distribution-style second layer sometimes beats a company's lower headline rate plus distribution tax, and sometimes doesn't — grounded in real advisory reasoning about capital raising, investor-preference, and exit-planning constraints, rather than a bare rate comparison table.
4. **Full-lifecycle depth in one page**, matching the pattern AU Corporate already executes well on `/india-business-setup/company-formation` (registration → FDI eligibility → process → documents → cost/timeline → compliance roadmap → FEMA/RBI → FAQ) rather than the fragmented pillar+spoke or single-topic patterns Stage 3 found among competitors.
5. **Visible authorship and currency signal** — matching the site's existing convention ("Last updated: [date] — prepared by AU Corporate's taxation and regulatory compliance practice," as used on `/india-business-setup/company-formation` and `/india-business-setup/fdi-channels`), which Stage 4 confirms zero analyzed competitors do, including the two advisory-positioned ones.

## Existing Site Overlap / Cannibalization Risk
- **`/india-business-setup/company-formation`** currently describes LLP in one line inside its entity-type comparison grid: "A hybrid structure combining partnership flexibility with limited liability; less commonly used for FDI-funded operating businesses than a Private Limited Company." The new LLP page must **extend, not contradict** this — it should explain the *why* behind that existing claim (tax treatment, capital-raising mechanics, investor preference) rather than repositioning LLP as equally common for FDI-funded operating businesses. Flag for the Content Architect: once this page exists, that entity-type card on `company-formation` should link to it (currently it doesn't link out to any LLP-specific page), and the new LLP page should link back to `company-formation` for the Pvt Ltd/WOS side of the comparison rather than re-explaining SPICe+ incorporation in full.
- **`/services/taxation-regulatory`** establishes the company-side tax rates (22% concessional, 25%/30% standard) but says nothing about LLP taxation at all — no conflict, but a genuine content gap this new page fills. The new page's tax section should reference this existing page for the company-rate comparison point rather than restating it independently, and should be precise that LLP taxation (flat 30%, Section 10(2A) single-level treatment) is a structurally different regime, not a variant of the company rates.
- **`/india-business-setup/fdi-channels`** documents the Automatic Route / Government Route framework, Press Note 3, and sector caps for FDI generally (framed around company share allotment). The new LLP page should link to it for the underlying automatic-route/government-route mechanics rather than re-explaining DPIIT/FIFP process steps, while being explicit that LLP capital contribution reporting (Form FDI-LLP(I)) is a different RBI filing from the FC-GPR filing that page and `company-formation` both document for companies. This is a complementary relationship, not competing content — but the distinction needs to be stated clearly so a reader doesn't assume the two filings are interchangeable.
- **`/india-business-setup/regulatory-compliance`** covers the six general compliance regimes (governance, labour, environment, FEMA, data protection, IP) at the company level. No direct conflict, but the LLP page's Form 8/Form 11/audit-threshold content is LLP-specific and shouldn't be duplicated there — link out rather than cross-post.
- **No standalone LLP registration/service page currently exists on the site.** This is a genuinely new page, not a rewrite, so the main risk isn't duplication — it's inconsistency with the tone and rate figures already committed to on the pages above. Any AMT rate, tax rate, or resident-day figure used here must match (or, where it's a distinct LLP-specific figure, be clearly flagged as such) rather than silently diverging from the company-side figures already published.

## Explicit Guidance for the Writer
- **Match the `company-formation` pillar's register exactly**: practical, procedural, first-person-firm ("what we most often see missed," "the honest answer sits across two figures") rather than promotional ("AU Corporate offers comprehensive LLP registration services"). That page never lists AU Corporate's services as a bullet menu — it explains mechanics and links to the relevant service page only where genuinely useful (e.g., "Talk to an expert →" micro-CTAs on process-step cards, one mid-page lead form, one FAQ-driven CTA cluster at the end). Follow that pattern, not a sales-page pattern.
- **Lead with the 120-day correction as a factual callout, not a boast.** State the current rule, cite the LLP (Amendment) Act, 2021, and note — factually, without gloating — that many sources still show the older 182-day figure. Avoid language like "unlike our competitors" or "we know better than other firms."
- **Keep the tax section comparative and reasoned, not just a rate table.** The value is in explaining *when* the 30% flat LLP rate is actually the better outcome versus when a company's concessional rate wins once distribution/exit mechanics are factored in — this is advisory reasoning, and it's the section most at risk of collapsing into a flat, promotional "LLP is great for tax" claim if not handled carefully. Keep it conditional and honest (as the company-formation page does with its "honest answer sits across two different figures" framing on cost/timeline).
- **Be precise about the FDI-LLP(I) filing vs. FC-GPR** — do not reuse or lightly edit the FC-GPR language from `company-formation`/`fdi-channels`; LLP capital contribution reporting is a different form and mechanic, and conflating them would be a factual error the site doesn't currently make elsewhere.
- **State the ₹40 lakh / ₹25 lakh audit threshold and any late-fee figures as commonly-cited compliance figures**, and flag internally (per Stage 1/4) that they were not independently verified against primary LLP Rules text in this pipeline — do not present them with more certainty than the sourcing supports. If a primary-source check is feasible before publishing, do it; otherwise phrase as "the threshold most commonly cited is..." rather than an unqualified statutory claim.
- **Do not turn the "full lifecycle in one page" structure into a service-line tour.** The lifecycle (registration → eligibility → FDI → tax → compliance → conversion) should be organized around the reader's actual sequence of questions, not around which AU Corporate department would handle each step. Service mentions belong only where they genuinely help — e.g., a brief note that ongoing Form 8/Form 11 filing and audit-threshold monitoring is the kind of thing worth having a retained compliance relationship for, stated once, not repeated as a CTA after every section.
- **Skip GST, transfer pricing, valuation, risk advisory, payroll, and Virtual CFO entirely** unless a specific reader question in the final content plan genuinely requires one of them — per the exclusions above, none of them are evidenced as needed for this page, and inserting any of them risks the page reading as an AU Corporate service-line inventory rather than an authoritative answer to "LLP in India."
- **Carry the same "Last updated" / authored-by-practice-area line** used on `company-formation` and `fdi-channels`, attributed to AU Corporate's taxation and regulatory compliance practice (the same practice area that owns LLP tax treatment, FDI eligibility, and annual compliance content elsewhere on the site) — this is a genuine trust signal per Stage 4, not decoration.
