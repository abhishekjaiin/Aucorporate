## Regulatory Currency Check

- The page's "last updated" line (29 September 2026) postdates every notification checked above (MCA Circular 08/2025, G.S.R. 943(E), G.S.R. 359(E), CCFS Circulars 01/2026 and 03/2026, MCA Circular 03/2025 on virtual AGMs) — no evidence found of the draft presenting an already-superseded rule as current, other than the two Must-Fix items above.
- **Material omission flagged:** the calendar's Income Tax row ("By 31 October, where applicable | Income tax return") does not clarify that companies with international related-party transactions — which, per the page's own adjacent Form 3CEB row, describes essentially every reader of this page (US-parent management fees, cost allocations, royalties) — get an extended ITR due date of **30 November**, not 31 October. As currently worded, a reader could reasonably take 31 October as their own company's ITR deadline when it is very likely 30 November for them. Recommend adding a clause distinguishing the two dates (e.g., "30 November where Form 3CEB / transfer-pricing reporting applies, which is the common case for a US-parented subsidiary") rather than leaving 31 October as the only figure shown.
- Minor imprecision, not a must-fix given the page explicitly defers full FC-GPR mechanics to the FEMA sub-page: "reports share allotment within 30 days of FDI coming in" slightly conflates two separate FEMA clocks — shares must be allotted within 60 days of receiving the funds, and FC-GPR is then due within 30 days of that allotment (not 30 days from receipt of funds). Recommend tightening the wording on a future pass; not a blocking item since the FEMA sub-page (out of this page's scope by design) presumably carries the precise mechanics.
- DIR-3 KYC's old annual cadence is correctly described throughout as superseded, not current. ADT-1's old first-auditor exemption is correctly described as changed, not standing. No stale/superseded process was found presented as still in effect.
- GST annual return (GSTR-9/9C) is not mentioned anywhere on the page. Given the page's own explicit, multi-stage-documented scope boundary (Companies Act/MCA-ROC calendar + thin FEMA cross-link + brief IT/TP mention, with GST, Labour Codes, DPDP, Environmental, and IP explicitly deferred to the sibling regulatory-compliance page per Stages 4-6's boundary decisions), this reads as a deliberate scope call rather than an oversight — flagged here for awareness only, not as a defect, since re-opening an explicitly-traced five-stage scope decision is outside this stage's mandate.
