## Edits Made

1. **Meta description shortened and re-led with the primary keyword.** Removed the opening "How to set up a " (Stage 6's draft ran 174 characters against the ~155-160 character band Stage 6 itself intended). New version is 158 characters and opens directly with "Project Office in India," improving both keyword prominence and truncation risk. No change to what the description promises. Updated the metadata note below it to reflect that this one field was edited (title and URL were left untouched).

2. **Split the intro's opening sentence for readability and snippet-tightness.** "A Project Office in India is the narrowest-purpose... here — used not for... entity." (one ~50-word sentence carrying two ideas) became two shorter sentences. This is a punctuation/structure change only — no wording, claims, or facts were altered — and produces a cleaner, more quotable direct-answer block immediately under the H1.

3. **Added one inline internal link to `/india-business-setup/fdi-channels`** in the "What Is a Project Office in India?" section, where the page already discusses the FEMA 22(R) statutory basis. Stage 6's content architecture specified this link should appear in body prose distinguishing the FEMA 22(R) establishment route from the FEMA 20(R) equity-investment route (matching the Liaison Office page's discipline on this point); the draft as handed off only had it in the closing Related Resources block. Anchor text ("The FDI Automatic vs Government Route") matches Stage 6's suggestion exactly.

No other changes were made. The UIN hedge (body Section 5 and FAQ #6), all Writer's Notes, and every factual claim were left untouched, per instruction.
