## Foreign/International Framing Check
**Evidence found for international/foreign-audience phrasing — and where the evidence stops:**
- **"Foreign subsidiary" / "foreign-owned"** phrasing IS evidence-backed: it recurs as literal page-title language across at least 9 independent competitor domains in both Stage 1's original research and this stage's confirmation queries (cleartax.in "Foreign Subsidiary Company Compliances in India"; mondaq.com "Key Compliances For Foreign Subsidiary Companies In India: A Checklist"; ahlawatassociates.com "Annual Compliance for Foreign Subsidiaries India"; registerkaro.in "Foreign Subsidiary Compliance for Indian Companies 2026-27"; incorpx.io "Foreign Subsidiary Compliance in India: Annual Filing Rules"; manishanilgupta.com "Year-End Compliance for Foreign-Owned Companies in India"; enterslice.com "Compliances Checklist for Foreign Subsidiary Company in India"; treelife.in "Foreign Subsidiary Compliance in India: A Guide for 2026"; estartindia.com "Annual compliances for foreign subsidiary"; companiesnext.com "Compliance for Subsidiary of Foreign Companies in India"; lexology.com "Annual Compliance for Foreign-Owned Indian Subsidiaries"). This is cross-domain recurrence, not a single source — it clears the bar this pipeline sets for letting "foreign" into the keyword itself. **This is the justified exception, and it's already broad-market foreign-subsidiary phrasing, not a narrower US-specific insertion.**
- **"US-owned" / "US company" / "US parent"** as a *compliance-calendar-specific* qualifier is **not evidence-backed**. No page title or recurring phrase in either Stage 1's 15-page competitor table or this stage's four confirmation queries used "US" as a qualifier for this topic. The only "US subsidiary" hits surfaced were a different intent entirely (entity-*registration* guides like "How to Register a US Subsidiary in India," and the reverse-direction "US Compliance Checklist for Indian Founders"). **STATUS.md's stated primary_keyword ("Indian subsidiary annual compliance for US companies") is AU's internal framing of its target buyer, not a confirmed search phrase** — flagging this explicitly per the pipeline's standing rule (foreign investor = audience, not automatically keyword).
- **Recommendation carried to Stage 3/5/6**: target "foreign subsidiary" / "foreign-owned" phrasing directly in the H1/title/meta (evidence-backed); address the US-specific angle through **content and audience framing** — an intro that names the US-parent scenario, examples using US dollar/US fiscal-year reconciliation, DIN-holding US director specifics, US-entity board-resolution mechanics — not through inserting "US" into the keyword itself. This mirrors how AU's own existing `/india-entry-for-us-companies/` hub already works: the URL cluster and audience are US-specific, but the ranking content underneath uses broad-market compliance terminology.
- No evidence (Semrush blocked; no WebSearch recurrence) supports inserting "cross-border" or "international" into any keyword for this topic. Do not use either term as a targeted keyword.
