## New Authoritative Information Found
All verified via WebSearch against primary/near-primary sources, not fabricated:
- **Companies Act, 2013, Section 2(87)** — defines "subsidiary company" by board control or majority voting power; a WOS is the 100%-ownership case of this definition, not a separately defined term. (Used to correct the old page's implicit framing of WOS as a distinct legal category.)
- **Companies Act, 2013, Section 149(3)** — resident director requirement (182 days in the financial year) — already cited on the pillar page; reused here specifically in the WOS/board-control context, not re-derived.
- **FEMA / DPIIT Consolidated FDI Policy sectoral caps and routes**: most sectors (manufacturing, most services, IT/software, most infrastructure) — 100% FDI, Automatic Route. Multi-brand retail trading — capped at 51%, Government Route only, no automatic-route option (confirmed via multiple independent advisory sources describing the same DPIIT policy position). Defence — up to 74% FDI under Automatic Route, Government Route required above that threshold.
- **100% FDI in insurance — effective 5 February 2026.** DPIIT Press Note No. 1 (2026 Series), dated 2 February 2026, amended the Consolidated FDI Policy, 2020 to permit 100% FDI under the Automatic Route in Indian insurance companies and insurance intermediaries; the corresponding Indian Insurance Companies (Foreign Investment) Amendment Rules, 2025 were notified 30 December 2025 and the Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha Act notification took effect 5 February 2026 (cross-confirmed via Mondaq, Lexology, Bar & Business, JSA). Prior cap was 74% under Automatic Route. This is a genuinely new development not on the old page and not commonly foregrounded in competitor WOS content.
- **RBI FC-GPR filing** — 30 days from the date of allotment (not fund receipt), filed via the FIRMS portal (Single Master Form), requiring FIRC, valuation certificate, KYC report, and a professional certificate (confirmed via multiple independent practitioner sources describing the same RBI/FEMA requirement).
- **Annual FLA Return** — due 15 July each year (provisional figures if unaudited, revised by 30 September), filed via RBI's FLAIR portal, standing obligation based on 31 March foreign-investment position regardless of in-year activity (confirmed via multiple independent sources).
- **Late FC-GPR consequence** — resolved via RBI's compounding process under FEMA (compounding application, RBI-calculated compounding amount), not a simple late fee — same mechanism already verified and cited on the pillar page this session; reused consistently here.
- **Rule 23, Foreign Exchange Management (Non-Debt Instruments) Rules, 2019 / FOCC concept** — a foreign-owned-or-controlled company's downstream investments are treated as indirect foreign investment subject to the same caps/routes — already verified and cited on the pillar page this session; reused consistently rather than re-derived, with a link to the pillar's fuller FOCC explainer to avoid duplicating that explanation.
- **Multi-brand retail FDI conditions** (51% cap, Government Route only, no automatic-route option) confirmed via Grant Thornton Bharat, ClearTax and other independent sources.
