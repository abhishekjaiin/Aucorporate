## The UIN Question — Resolved, With an Important Caveat on How

This was the highest-priority item, and I was able to make real progress on it, though not full primary-source confirmation.

**What I found, and how strong it is:**

1. **Nishith Desai Associates' FAQ** (a high-authority law firm source) states directly and unambiguously: *"No Unique Identification Number (UIN) is allotted to a Project Office (PO)."* This is a clean, direct, unhedged statement from a credible authority, repeated identically across multiple independent search results referencing the same PDF.

2. Independent of the Nishith Desai source, I ran several searches specifically targeting the **RBI/AD Category-I bank Master Direction's own operative paragraph** on UIN allotment (the paragraph describing the AD bank forwarding Form FNC to the General Manager, RBI, for UIN allotment before issuing the approval letter). Across multiple, independently-scraped citations of what appears to be the same Master Direction paragraph (via taxguru.in, ipandlegalfilings.com, and others, none of which reference each other), the operative sentence is consistently rendered as **"for allotment of Unique Identification Number (UIN) to each BO/LO"** and **"the AD Category-I bank shall issue the approval letter to the non-resident entity for establishing BO/LO in India"** — i.e., the UIN-allotment sentence itself, as quoted across independent citing sources, names only BO/LO, not BO/LO/PO. This recurring, specific phrasing pattern (not a generic paraphrase, but the same two-clause sentence structure appearing near-verbatim across unrelated sites) is the strongest evidence I found, because it looks like independent partial quotation of the same primary text rather than independent editorial conclusions.

3. Several other secondary/company-formation sources (setindiabiz.com, masllp.com, carajput.com, cagmc.com, gkkediaandco.com) describe UIN allotment as happening for a Project Office via the same Form FNC/AD-bank route as BO/LO — but in every case I checked, these read like generic templates that describe the BO/LO/PO application route as a single undifferentiated process without engaging with the UIN question as a distinct sub-issue the way Nishith Desai and the Master Direction quotations above do. None of them cite a primary source for the specific claim that PO gets a UIN; they appear to be extrapolating the BO/LO rule across all three structures.

4. Separately, and consistent with all of the above: the six-month-opening-window sources describe that window as applying across BO/LO/PO collectively (not carving PO out), which is a useful control — it shows that where a rule genuinely does apply to all three structures, the sources say so explicitly ("BO/LO/PO"), which makes the recurring "BO/LO"-only phrasing in the UIN-specific paragraph more likely to be a real, deliberate distinction rather than an accident of paraphrasing.

**My conclusion:** The weight of evidence — a specific, high-authority, unhedged legal-FAQ statement, corroborated by a recurring and specifically-scoped ("BO/LO," not "BO/LO/PO") quotation pattern from what appears to be the Master Direction's own operative UIN paragraph, against a set of generic company-formation-site claims that don't individually engage with the distinction — points toward **a Project Office not being allotted a UIN, unlike a Branch or Liaison Office.** This is a real, sourced conclusion, not a coin flip.

**But I want to be precise about its limits:** I was never able to WebFetch rbi.org.in, rbidocs.rbi.org.in, or any mirror of the Master Direction directly — every attempt returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED`. Everything above comes from WebSearch snippets, which involve a layer of search-engine synthesis I cannot fully audit. I am confident enough to make a call, but this is **convergent secondary evidence, clearly labeled as such — not primary-source-confirmed** in the sense of my having read rbi.org.in's own text.
