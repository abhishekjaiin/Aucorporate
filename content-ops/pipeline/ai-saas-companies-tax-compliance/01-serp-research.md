## Recurring Entities & Regulations
India-side (concrete, named repeatedly on ranking pages):
- **OIDAR** (Online Information and Database Access or Retrieval) — the central India-specific regulatory hook; definition was amended in October 2023 to broaden scope and remove the "minimal human intervention" qualifier, explicitly extending to cloud computing, AI, virtual currencies, online advertising and education content delivered from outside India. This is confirmed across multiple independently-recurring sources (india-briefing.com, Fonoa, ClearTax, Grant Thornton India, CashFlo).
- **GSTR-5A** — the monthly return non-resident OIDAR providers must file, due the 20th of the following month, even as a nil return.
- **Non-Resident Taxable Person (NRTP)** and **Form GST REG-09 / GST REG-10** — registration mechanics for non-resident suppliers.
- **NTOR** (Non-Taxable Online Recipient) — the B2C-recipient concept that triggers direct foreign-supplier GST liability, vs. **Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM)** for B2B supplies where the Indian registered recipient self-assesses IGST.
- **Section 122, CGST Act** (penalty for failure to register) and **Section 50** (18% p.a. interest on unpaid IGST) — cited specifically on the non-registration-penalty query.
- **Equalisation Levy** (Finance Act 2016, Chapter VIII) — the 2% levy was withdrawn August 2024; the 6% levy on digital advertising was withdrawn effective April 1, 2025 (Finance Bill 2025). Multiple sources note this was framed as alignment with OECD Pillar One, not solely a response to US tariff pressure. Any page must reflect that this levy is now gone, not describe it as current.
- **Significant Economic Presence (SEP)** — Finance Act 2018 concept; CBDT-notified thresholds: INR 2 crore (~USD 240,000) in transactions with Indian users per financial year, or 300,000 Indian users per financial year, creating a "business connection" for foreign digital companies even with SEP repeal of equalisation levy still leaving SEP/withholding exposure live.
- **FEMA, 1999** and RBI filings: **FC-GPR** (within 30 days of share allotment to a non-resident), **FC-TRS**, **FLA** annual return, **APR** — via the **FIRMS portal**.
- **Companies Act, 2013**, **MCA** (RUN name-reservation service, DIN, DSC, incorporation filing), **Wholly Owned Subsidiary (WOS)** as the consistently-cited default structure for SaaS/tech entry (cited as "90%+" or "most preferred" choice across several sources — not independently verified, presented as a repeated claim, not a checked statistic).
- SAC Code **9983** for SaaS under GST; standard GST rate cited consistently as **18%**.

Global-side (recurring on the VAT/GST-on-digital-services thread, mostly on non-India-focused pages):
- **EU VAT OSS (One Stop Shop)** — €10,000 cross-border B2C threshold, quarterly return via single member-state registration.
- **UK VAT** post-Brexit treated as separate from EU VAT compliance.
- **Canada GST/HST** provincial-vs-federal complexity cited as a comparison point.
- OECD **Pillar One** referenced as the broader global framework India's Equalisation Levy repeal is aligning with.
