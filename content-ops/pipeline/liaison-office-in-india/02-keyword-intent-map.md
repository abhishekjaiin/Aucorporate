## Foreign/International Framing Check
No PAA, related-search, or competitor title in Stage 1's research uses the literal words "foreign," "international," "overseas," or "cross-border" in the head term or secondary phrasing — every ranking title uses plain "liaison office in India" / "liaison office registration India" language, even though a Liaison Office is *definitionally* only ever established by a company incorporated outside India (this is a structural fact of the entity type under FEMA, not a targeting choice). This mirrors the Branch Office page's precedent: the audience is inherently foreign, but the query is not.

**Conclusion: No search-demand evidence for foreign/international framing in the keyword itself.** Target broad-market queries ("liaison office in India," "liaison office registration in India," "RBI approval liaison office India"). Address the foreign-parent/international-investor angle through content and audience framing (e.g., "if your company is incorporated outside India...") and through FAQ answers to genuinely foreign-specific sub-questions (e.g., the Nepal-only-LO rule, Pakistan/Bangladesh/China special handling) — not by inserting "foreign" or "international" into the primary or secondary keyword strings.
