## Current Page vs. Current SERP
WebSearch access was intermittent during this session (several queries returned "web search unavailable"), and direct fetches of top-ranking competitor pages (IndiaFilings, RegisterKaro) were blocked by the network egress proxy, so SERP analysis here is based on the queries that did return results plus cross-referencing the site's own already-published, already-verified figures on `/india-business-setup/company-formation` and `/india-business-setup/fdi-channels`.

What the successful SERP query ("India company registration cost breakdown private limited company 2026") surfaced that the old page didn't have at all:
- A two-bucket cost framing (government/statutory fees vs. professional/advisory fees) — the old page presented six flat rupee ranges with no explanation of what was inside them or why they varied.
- State-level stamp duty as the largest cost variable (ranging from near-nil in some states to several thousand rupees in others) — entirely absent from the old page.
- The SPICe+ government filing fee waiver for authorised capital up to ₹15 lakh — a specific, checkable regulatory fact competitors lead with; the old page had no regulatory grounding for its cost figures at all.
- Recurring annual compliance costs (audit, ROC filings, ITR, GST returns) presented as a distinct, ongoing budget line separate from one-time setup cost — the old page implied the six line items were the entire cost of doing business in India, with nothing about what happens after year one.
- Explicit "why timelines slip" content (name rejection, DSC delay, apostille turnaround) addressing the real search intent behind "how long does it take," which is usually "why is this taking longer than the number I read," not just the number itself.

None of this required inventing new facts — it aligned closely with logic already fully worked out and verified elsewhere on the site (the delay-cause list, the 4-6/8-12 week route figures, the FLA/FC-GPR/resident-director points) on `/india-business-setup/company-formation`, `/india-business-setup/fdi-channels`, `/india-business-setup/regulatory-compliance` and `/india-business-setup/banking-taxation` — it just hadn't been pulled together on this specific page.

## Search Intent Assessment
**Unchanged, but under-served.** This remains a purely informational query ("how long does it take" / "what does it cost") — no evidence surfaced of new commercial competitors or a shift toward transactional intent that would require restructuring the page around comparison shopping or pricing tables. The gap was depth and specificity, not direction: the page answered the right question but gave almost no reasoning, context, or completeness behind the six numbers and five timeline rows it had. The expansion adds the "why" behind each figure and the recurring-cost dimension competitors already cover, without changing what the page is fundamentally about.

## Not Changed (and why)
- **The five timeline week-ranges and six budget rupee figures themselves** — left numerically identical. They're already consistent with the 4-6/8-12 week figures verified and published elsewhere on the site, and the audit finding was about missing explanation and depth, not incorrect numbers. Changing them without a specific trigger (a regulatory change to fees, or a documented inaccuracy) would violate the "don't invent unverifiable figures" guardrail.
- **Full page rewrite** — considered and rejected. The existing skeleton (timeline list + cost grid + CTA) matches what a user searching this topic actually wants; the fix was adding the missing explanatory layer and two missing sections (state variance, annual recurring costs), not replacing the structure.
- **State-by-state stamp duty table with specific rupee figures per state** — deliberately left qualitative rather than quantitative. The SERP corroboration for specific state figures wasn't verifiable against a primary source in this session (mca.gov.in and individual state stamp department sites were not fetchable through the available tools), so the page states the directional pattern (near-nil in some states, several thousand rupees in others) without asserting precise numbers per state that couldn't be independently confirmed.
- **`app/sitemap.ts`** — not touched, per explicit instruction; `lastModified` bump left for manual handling after diff review.
